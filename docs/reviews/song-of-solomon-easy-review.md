### Format-change check (item-by-item, per the Leviticus/Proverbs-easy precedent that format is not neutral)

interpretive question the `medium` brief's plainer recall format avoided:

- **Item 2** ("how many times does the adjuration appear in the book?") and **item 10** ("which
  daughters are addressed?") are new *structural-fact* questions not present in `medium` at all —
  checked specifically since these are genuinely new question types, not just reused verses in a
  new wrapper. Both test countable/nameable facts stated directly by the passage list itself (the
  refrain occurs at 2:7, 3:5, 8:4 — three times; addressed to "the daughters of Jerusalem" in all
  three). Neither requires characterizing the refrain's meaning or the relationship between
  occurrences. **No new contested ground.**
- **Items 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8** recast `medium`'s or one another's already-graded facts into MC
  recognition format. Each checked individually: none moves the graded token onto contested
  material (e.g. item 7's graded answer is "death," from the plain first sentence of 8:6, not
  "Sheol" or "Yah," which stay outside this item's display text entirely). **No new contested
  ground.**
- **Item 9** (boss sequence) orders passages by where they are *first encountered*, not by asserting
  any thematic relationship between them, matching the same "ordering only, no characterization"
  pattern already validated for Ecclesiastes' equivalent boss item. **No new contested ground.**

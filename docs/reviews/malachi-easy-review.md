## 3. Contested-Territory Cross-Check

### 3.1 Re-confirming the two full exclusions (1:2-3, 2:10-16)

Every item's citation (1:7, 1:11, 3:1, 3:3, 3:9, 3:17, 4:1, 4:4, 4:5, and the reused 6-passage
sequence) falls within the 6 already-approved passages, none of which touch 1:2-3 or 2:10-16.
**Mechanical scope check — passes.**

### 3.2 Malachi 3:1 (item 3) and 3:3 (item 4) — re-confirming the identity-neutral bounding

Item 3 grades only "what will Yahweh's messenger do" (prepare the way) — a stated action, not an
identity claim about "my messenger," "the Lord," or "the messenger of the covenant." Item 4 grades
only "who will the coming refiner purify" (the sons of Levi) — again a stated action/outcome, not who
the refiner is. Both confirmed to sit inside the exact bounding already established in
`docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.3: "the described actions... stand on their own as narrative-style
content independent of identity." **Bounding holds for both items.**

### 3.3 Malachi 3:9 (item 5) — re-confirming the recall-only/no-modern-application bounding

Grades only the plain historical charge stated in 3:8-9 (the whole nation is robbing Yahweh in tithes
and offerings) — no assertion about whether this instruction binds present-day readers. Confirmed
inside the same bounding already established in `docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.4. **Bounding
holds.**

### 3.4 Malachi 4:5 (item 9) — re-confirming the no-fulfillment-claim bounding

Grades only what the text names — "Elijah the prophet" — and when he is promised (before the great
and terrible day). No claim about fulfillment, timing, or identity beyond the text's own words.
Confirmed inside the exact bounding already established in `docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.6,
including that review's finding that "the text of Malachi 4:5 itself names its figure explicitly...
with zero ambiguity about which historical prophet is meant." **Bounding holds.**

### 3.5 New verses/clauses checked independently for this tier

- **Malachi 1:7 (item 1)** — the priests' polluted-bread offering, plain narrative fact, no
  cross-tradition dispute; part of the already-cleared 1:6-11 passage. **No concern.**
- **Malachi 1:11 (item 2)** — Yahweh's name being great "from the rising of the sun to its going
  down." Confirmed this is universalizing prophetic language about the reach of Yahweh's name, not a
  disputed identity or fulfillment claim; already part of the cleared 1:6-11 passage and already used
  as a recall item in `medium` with no concern raised there. **No concern.**
- **Malachi 3:17 (item 6)** — Yahweh's promise to spare those who feared him "as a man spares his own
  son." Tender covenantal imagery, plain direct quotation, part of the already-cleared 3:13-18
  passage. **No concern.**
- **Malachi 4:1 (item 7)** — the day of judgment burning the wicked "like stubble." Part of the
  already-cleared 4:1-3 passage, purely descriptive judgment imagery with the same no-identity-claim
  bounding already applied to that passage in `docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.5. **No concern.**
- **Malachi 4:4 (item 8)** — "remember the law of Moses." Plain historical instruction, uncontested;
  part of the already-cleared 4:4-6 passage. **No concern.**

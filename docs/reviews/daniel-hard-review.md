## 3. Contested-Territory Cross-Check

This tier introduces no new scenes and stays within the same six chapter-1-6 scenes medium already
scoped. This review confirmed, scene by scene, that every new item falls inside its scene's chapter
and does not draw on chapters 7-12 or on Daniel 4:33.

- **Chapters 7-12 as a whole.** Checked the brief file text directly: no verse, scene description,
  or reference to any content from these chapters appears anywhere, including in the boss
  quotation-matching item (item 10), which draws all three quotations from chapters 1, 3, and 6.
  **Exclusion confirmed complete.**
- **Daniel 2, the dream's kingdom-identification.** Item 2 (2:23) is Daniel's own words of thanks
  for the dream's revelation to him — never a question about which kingdom any part of the statue
  represents. **Rule honored, confirmed** — and this tier's recall-by-default design makes the
  restriction naturally satisfied.
- **Daniel 4, Nebuchadnezzar's madness.** Item 4 (4:35) is drawn from the restoration/praise
  passage (4:34-37 territory), the same restrained register the medium brief uses for this scene —
  **not** from 4:33, which remains excluded from challenge-ready text at every tier, consistent
  with `docs/content/daniel.md`'s and `docs/content/daniel-hard.md`'s own explicit statement of
  this exclusion. **Rule honored, confirmed.**
- **Daniel 5, temple vessels and idol-praise language.** Item 5 (5:23) is Daniel's own recounting of
  Belshazzar's offense (praising gods of silver, gold, bronze, iron, wood, and stone) — the medium
  review already found this uncontested narrative fact across the traditions checked, and this item
  tests only the verse's own wording, never a doctrinal claim about idolatry. **Rule honored,
  confirmed.**
- **Daniel 6, the den of lions.** No contested-territory concern identified at medium; items 6 and 7
  (6:10, 6:23) and boss item 8's reuse of 6:22 are plain narrative-fact recall, consistent with that
  finding. **Confirmed, no overlap.**

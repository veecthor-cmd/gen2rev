## 3. Contested-Territory Cross-Check

### 3.1 Re-confirming the four full exclusions (9:9, 11:12-13, 12:10, 13:7)

Every item's citation (1:1, 1:5, 1:6, 3:2, 3:4, 4:7, 5:4, 8:6, 8:8, and the reused 7-passage sequence)
falls within the 7 already-approved chs. 1-8 passages. **Mechanical scope check — passes.**

### 3.2 Re-confirming the "Branch" bounding (3:8-10, 6:12-13)

Item 3 (3:2) and item 4 (3:4) both fall inside the already-approved 3:1-5 range, well short of the
"Branch" material at 3:8. Chapter 6 is not used anywhere in this brief. **Bounding holds.**

### 3.3 Zechariah 3:2 (item 3) — independently checked, new item

This item grades Yahweh's rebuke of Satan and its stated reason ("Yahweh who has chosen Jerusalem")
— narrative fact and direct quotation only. Confirmed this sits squarely inside the same "Satan"
bounding already established in `docs/reviews/zechariah-review.md` §3.7: the item does not ask what
"Satan" is or does beyond the text's own words, and "Jerusalem" here is a plain narrative reference
(Yahweh's chosen city, already established narrative fact from the passage's own setting, not a
disputed identity claim). **No concern.**

### 3.4 Zechariah 3:4's second clause (item 4) — independently checked

`Medium` already used this verse's first sentence ("take the filthy garments off him") as an MC item,
already cleared. This item uses the second sentence ("I have caused your iniquity to pass from you,
and I will clothe you with rich clothing") for a recall blank on "rich." Confirmed this is the same
narrative content already reviewed as part of the whole 3:1-5 passage — restoration and re-clothing,
narrative fact only, no atonement-theology framing added (the brief does not ask what "iniquity
passing" means theologically, only recalls the word "rich" describing the clothing). **No concern.**

### 3.5 Zechariah 8:6 (item 7) — independently checked, new item

Yahweh's rhetorical self-reference ("should it also be marvelous in my eyes?"). Checked independently:
this is plain first-person prophetic speech about Yahweh's own reaction to the restoration he is
describing — not a pronoun-ambiguity question of the kind flagged for Genesis 1:26, and not adjacent
to any of the identity-contested passages in chs. 9-14. No live cross-tradition disagreement found or
expected on a verse that is purely rhetorical self-reference. **No concern.**

### 3.6 Remaining new items (1, 2, 5, 6, 8, 10) — independently checked

- **Zechariah 1:1 (item 1)** — the book's dating notice (month, year, king's reign, prophet's
  genealogy). Purely historical/narrative detail, uncontested. **No concern.**
- **Zechariah 1:5 (item 2)** — rhetorical question about mortality ("your fathers, where are they?").
  Plain narrative rhetoric, no doctrinal content. **No concern.**
- **Zechariah 4:7 (item 5)** — continuation of the Zerubbabel oracle, purely descriptive/celebratory
  ("Grace, grace"). Already implicitly covered by the prior review's clearance of the whole 4:1-7
  passage as safe (`docs/reviews/zechariah-review.md` §1, "Match, two independent sources" for the
  full 4:1-7 range). **No concern.**
- **Zechariah 5:4 (item 6)** — continuation of the flying-scroll vision's stated meaning (the curse
  entering a guilty household). Plain narrative/symbolic description with a stated meaning already
  given in-text, same category as the rest of the already-cleared 5:1-4 passage. **No concern.**
- **Zechariah 1:6 (item 8)** — the people's own reported response ("Yahweh determined to do to us...
  according to our practices"). Plain narrative fact (what the people said), already part of the
  cleared 1:1-6 passage. **No concern.**
- **Zechariah 8:8 (item 10)** — the closing line of the restoration oracle ("I will be their God, in
  truth and in righteousness"), part of the already-cleared 8:3-8 passage. **No concern.**

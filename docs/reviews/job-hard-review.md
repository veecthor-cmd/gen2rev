## 3. Contested-Territory Cross-Check

This tier introduces no new scenes, no new passages, and no verse ranges beyond what the medium
brief already scoped. This review confirmed, verse range by verse range, that every new item stays
inside the seven ranges medium defined (Job 1:1-3, 1:6-12, 1:13-22, 2:7-10, 38:1-7, 38:16-18,
38:31-33, 39:19-25, 42:1-8, 42:10-17, 19:25-27) — none extends into Job 2:1-6, Job 38:8-30/34-41,
Job 39:1-18/26-30, Job 40:1-5, or any other adjacent material medium did not scope. This matters
more for this brief than most, since Job's medium review specifically flagged verse-range precision
as load-bearing for how the exclusions hold together.

- **"The Satan" figure (Job 1:6-12).** Item 2 (1:9-10) is verbatim recall of Satan's dialogue only.
  **Rule honored, confirmed.**
- **Job 1:21.** Item 3 is recall/fill-in-blank only (a different blank than `medium`/`easy`), never
  multiple choice. **Rule honored, confirmed.**
- **Job 19:25-27.** Item 8 (19:26) is recall/fill-in-blank only; boss item 10 quotes 42:6 (not
  19:25-27) for matching, not interpretation. **Rule honored, confirmed** — no item in this brief
  asks what "Redeemer" means or who the Redeemer is.
- **Job 42:6.** Item used only as a verbatim quotation in the boss matching item (item 10, "place in
  the order they occur and match each to its reference") — this tests sequence/attribution, not
  interpretation of the line's meaning, consistent with the medium brief's restriction. **Rule
  honored, confirmed.**
- **Job 42:7 (God's rebuke of the friends).** Appears only as a narrative-summary label in the boss
  sequence (item 9: "Yahweh rebukes the friends and restores Job twofold") — states that the rebuke
  happened, never why. **Rule honored, confirmed.**
- **Deliberately excluded material (Job 3-37; Behemoth/Leviathan, 40:15-41:34; Job 1:18-19).**
  Verified by re-reading every item, including the boss sequence's narrative labels, against all
  three exclusion ranges — no overlap found. The boss sequence (item 9) summarizes "Job loses
  everything in a single day" without citing 1:18-19, matching the medium brief's own precedent.
  **Confirmed, no overlap found.**
- **Core assignment guardrail.** No item in this brief asks the player to affirm or reject a
  position on why God allows suffering, or whether Job's friends' theology was right or wrong.
  **Confirmed.**

## Decision values

- `submitted` — brief written, awaiting agent review
- `approved` — clears the gate, eligible for content-pipeline ingestion
- `approved-with-changes` — approved after specific revisions; note the revision in this row
- `rejected` — not eligible to ship; must be revised and resubmitted as a new row
- `escalate-to-human` — agent found something it isn't confident adjudicating alone; **blocked**
  until a named human reviewer exists to receive it (see gap note above)

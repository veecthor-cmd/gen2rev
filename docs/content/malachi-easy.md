## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
Malachi's 6 oracular passages and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. It does
**not** simplify or paraphrase verse text — the style guide's "verbatim WEB text only" rule applies
identically at every tier. All items are `difficulty_rank` 1.

**This tier also keeps Malachi 4:5's Elijah promise as a closing/boss-adjacent beat**, consistent
with `medium`'s own treatment of that verse as this world's — and this whole product's OT arc's —
capstone (see `docs/content/malachi.md`'s "Capstone note"), even though only the hard tier's
capstone placement was explicitly required by this assignment.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/malachi.md`, no new passages added

Same 6 passages: Malachi 1:6-11 (Corrupt Offerings), 3:1-3 (The Coming Messenger), 3:6-10 ("Will a

Law; Elijah's Return). **Malachi 1:2-3 and 2:10-16 remain entirely out of scope at this tier, same

## Challenge Items (10: 6 regular + 4 boss)

### Regular (6)

1. **Malachi 1:7** (WEB, new): "You offer polluted bread on my altar. You say, 'How have we polluted
   you?' In that you say, 'Yahweh's table is contemptible.'" — MC: "According to Malachi 1:7, what
   did the priests offer on Yahweh's altar that he calls polluted?" Options: bread / wine / oil /
   incense. Correct: bread.
2. **Malachi 1:11** (already-approved verse, new format): "For from the rising of the sun even to
   its going down, my name is great among the nations, and in every place incense will be offered to
   my name, and a pure offering; for my name is great among the nations," says Yahweh of Armies." —
   MC: "According to Malachi 1:11, from where to where would Yahweh's name be great among the
   nations?" Options: "from the rising of the sun to its going down" / "from Jerusalem to Babylon" /
   "from Egypt to Persia" / "from the mountains to the sea." Correct: from the rising of the sun to
   its going down.
3. **Malachi 3:1** (already-approved verse, new format): "Behold, I send my messenger, and he will
   prepare the way before me! The Lord, whom you seek, will suddenly come to his temple. Behold, the
   messenger of the covenant, whom you desire, is coming!" says Yahweh of Armies." — MC: "According
   to Malachi 3:1, what will Yahweh's messenger do?" Options: "prepare the way before him" / "build
   the temple" / "gather the exiles" / "write the Law." Correct: prepare the way before him.
4. **Malachi 3:3** (WEB, new): "and he will sit as a refiner and purifier of silver, and he will
   purify the sons of Levi, and refine them as gold and silver; and they shall offer to Yahweh
   offerings in righteousness." — MC: "According to Malachi 3:3, who will the coming refiner
   purify?" Options: "the sons of Levi" / "the sons of Judah" / "all the nations" / "the elders of
   Israel." Correct: the sons of Levi. *(Grades only what the text says will happen — a refiner will
   purify the sons of Levi — never who "the Lord"/"the messenger of the covenant" is, per the
   existing 3:1-5 identity-neutral bounding below.)*
5. **Malachi 3:9** (WEB, new): "You are cursed with the curse; for you rob me, even this whole
   nation." — MC: "According to Malachi 3:9, who did Yahweh say was robbing him?" Options: "this
   whole nation" / "the priests alone" / "the wealthy landowners" / "the foreign nations." Correct:
   this whole nation.
6. **Malachi 3:17** (WEB, new): "They shall be mine," says Yahweh of Armies, "my own possession in
   the day that I make. I will spare them, as a man spares his own son who serves him." — MC:
   "According to Malachi 3:17, how would Yahweh spare those who feared him?" Options: "as a man
   spares his own son who serves him" / "as a king spares his loyal soldiers" / "as a shepherd spares
   his flock" / "as a judge spares the innocent." Correct: as a man spares his own son who serves
   him.

### Boss (4)

7. **Malachi 4:1** (WEB, new; trimmed for challenge use — full verse: "For behold, the day comes,
   burning like a furnace, when all the proud and all who work wickedness will be stubble. The day
   that comes will burn them up," says Yahweh of Armies, "so that it will leave them neither root nor
   branch."): "...when all the proud and all who work wickedness will be stubble." — recall: "...when
   all the proud and all who work wickedness will be ___." → **stubble**.
8. **Malachi 4:4** (already-approved verse, new format): "Remember the law of Moses my servant, which
   I commanded to him in Horeb for all Israel, even statutes and ordinances." — MC: "According to
   Malachi 4:4, whose law were the people told to remember?" Options: "the law of Moses" / "the law
   of the prophets" / "the law of the king" / "the law of Aaron." Correct: the law of Moses.
9. **Malachi 4:5** (already-approved verse, new format — this world's closing beat): "Behold, I will
   send you Elijah the prophet before the great and terrible day of Yahweh comes." — MC: "According
   to Malachi 4:5, who did Yahweh promise to send before the great and terrible day?" Options:
   "Elijah the prophet" / "Moses the lawgiver" / "an angel of the covenant" / "a king from David's
   line." Correct: Elijah the prophet. *(Grades only what the text names — no fulfillment claim, no
   identity question beyond "Elijah the prophet" as the text itself says — per the existing 4:4-6
   bounding below.)*
10. **Boss sequence — reused verbatim from `medium`**: Order the pivotal-passage blocks as they
    appear in the book of Malachi (Corrupt offerings → the coming messenger → tithing → the book of
    remembrance → the day of Yahweh → Elijah's return).

*(Items 2, 3, 8, and 9 reuse already-reviewed verses from `medium` in new MC format, per the pilot's
own Genesis-easy precedent — deliberately, to keep the book's most quoted lines present at every
tier, with item 9 specifically preserving the Elijah promise as this world's closing/boss-adjacent
beat. Item 10 is an exact reuse of an already-reviewed `medium` boss item. Every other item (1, 4, 5,
6, 7) is a genuinely new item drawn from a verse the `medium` tier's 17 items never used standalone.)*

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/malachi.md` applies unchanged and is **re-confirmed to
hold for this tier**:

- **No verse from Malachi 1:2-3 ("Jacob I loved, but Esau I hated") appears anywhere in this brief.**
- **No verse from Malachi 2:10-16 (unfaithfulness and divorce) appears anywhere in this brief.**
- **Malachi 3:1 and 3:3 (items 3 and 4)** stay strictly on identity-neutral action content — a
  messenger will come, the coming refiner purifies the sons of Levi — never on who "my messenger,"
  "the Lord," or "the messenger of the covenant" are, per the existing bounding in
  `docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.3.
- **Malachi 3:9 (item 5)** stays on the plain historical charge (the nation is robbing Yahweh in
  tithes and offerings, per 3:8) with no assertion about present-day applicability, per the existing
  recall-only/no-modern-application bounding for 3:6-12 in `docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.4.
- **Malachi 4:5 (item 9)** names "Elijah the prophet" exactly as the text does and asserts nothing
  about fulfillment, timing, or identity beyond that — per the existing bounding for 4:4-6 in
  `docs/reviews/malachi-review.md` §3.6.
- **No new contested ground otherwise.** Malachi 1:7, 1:11, 3:17, and 4:1 are plain narrative
  description or direct quotation with no live cross-tradition disagreement identified.

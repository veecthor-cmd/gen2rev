## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple," following the Genesis pilot's design intent
(`docs/content/genesis-easy.md`). This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
Esther's 5 scenes and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall, so a first-time player
can succeed without prior Bible knowledge. All items in this brief sit at **`difficulty_rank` 1**.
It does **not** simplify or paraphrase verse text — verbatim WEB text only, identical rule to every
other tier.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/esther.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes as `medium`: (1) Esther Becomes Queen (Esther 2), (2) Haman's Plot — The Decree
Against the Jews (Esther 3), (3) "For Such a Time as This" — Esther's Decision (Esther 4),
(4) Esther's Banquets and Haman's Downfall (Esther 5-7), (5) Reversal and the Establishment of
Purim (Esther 8-9).

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Esther 2:17** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format — **recall/fill-in-blank only, per the
   medium brief's structural rule below, never multiple-choice**): "The king loved Esther more than
   all the women, and she obtained favor and kindness in his sight more than all the virgins; so
   that he set the royal crown on her head, and made her queen instead of ___." → **Vashti**.
2. **Esther 2:5** (WEB, new): "There was a certain Jew in the citadel of Susa whose name was
   Mordecai, the son of Jair, the son of Shimei, the son of Kish, a Benjamite." — MC: "What was the
   name of the cousin who raised Esther?" Options: Mordecai / Haman / Hegai / Ahasuerus. Correct:
   Mordecai.
3. **Esther 3:2** (WEB, new; trimmed for challenge use — full verse: "All the king's servants who
   were in the king's gate bowed down and paid homage to Haman, for the king had so commanded
   concerning him. But Mordecai didn't bow down or pay him homage."): "...But Mordecai didn't bow
   down or pay him homage." — MC: "Who refused to bow down to Haman?" Options: Mordecai / Esther /
   the king / Hegai. Correct: Mordecai.
4. **Esther 3:13** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle; trimmed — full verse: "Letters
   were sent by couriers into all the king's provinces, to destroy, to kill, and to cause to perish,
   all Jews, both young and old, little children and women, in one day..."): "Letters were sent by
   couriers into all the king's provinces, to destroy, to kill, and to cause to perish, all Jews..."
   — MC: "What did Haman's decree order against the Jewish people?" Options: to destroy them / to
   tax them heavily / to exile them / to enslave them. Correct: to destroy them. *(Narrative-fact
   only, matching the medium brief's own use of this verse — no item asks whether the decree or its
   reversal was justified.)*
5. **Esther 4:16** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Go, gather together all the
   Jews who are present in Susa, and fast for me, and neither eat nor drink three days, night or
   day. I and my maidens will also fast the same way. Then I will go in to the king, which is
   against the law; and if I perish, I perish." — MC: "What was Esther willing to risk by going to
   the king uninvited?" Options: her own life / her position as queen only / nothing, it was safe /
   her wealth. Correct: her own life.
6. **Esther 8:16** (WEB, new): "The Jews had light, gladness, joy, and honor." — MC: "How did the
   Jews feel when the king's counter-decree was announced?" Options: light, gladness, joy, and honor
   / afraid and confused / angry and vengeful / indifferent. Correct: light, gladness, joy, and
   honor.
7. **Esther 9:26** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Therefore they called these
   days 'Purim,' from the word 'Pur.'" — MC: "What is the name of the festival established to
   remember the Jews' deliverance?" Options: Purim / Passover / Hanukkah / Sukkot. Correct: Purim.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/esther.md` applies unchanged: the absence of God's name
from the text; the "gathering of virgins" / royal harem context; the genocide plot and its violent
reversal. **No new contested ground for this tier** — every new verse (2:5, 3:2, 8:16, and the
already-approved verses reused with new question angles) is plain narrative description already
covered by the medium brief's analysis.

**Structural rules carried forward, confirmed to hold for this tier:**
- **Esther 2:7, 2:15, and 2:17 remain restricted to recall/fill-in-blank format only, never
  multiple-choice.** This tier's only use of a restricted verse (item 1, Esther 2:17) is authored
  as recall, per that rule — the easy tier's general preference for multiple-choice does not
  override this book-specific structural rule.
- **Esther 9:6-10 (Haman's ten sons named individually) and Esther 9:16 (the 75,000 casualty
  figure) remain excluded from all challenge-ready content at this tier, as at every tier.** No item
  above draws on either passage; item 4 (3:13) and item 8 (7:9-10) stay at the same
  narrative-summary level the medium brief already established for this material.

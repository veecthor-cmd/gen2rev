## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge," following the Genesis pilot's design
intent (`docs/content/genesis-hard.md`). This tier draws on less commonly memorized, more precise
clauses from within the same six already-approved scenes, and requires typed recall (no
multiple-choice scaffolding) for every regular item. All items in this brief sit at
**`difficulty_rank` 5**. Same guardrails as every other tier: verbatim WEB text only, narrative
fact only, no doctrinal position required.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/daniel.md`, no new scenes added, all within ch. 1-6

Same 6 scenes: (1) Daniel and His Friends Refuse the King's Food (Dan 1); (2) Nebuchadnezzar's
Dream and Daniel's Interpretation (Dan 2); (3) The Fiery Furnace (Dan 3); (4) Nebuchadnezzar's
Pride and Restoration (Dan 4); (5) Belshazzar's Feast and the Writing on the Wall (Dan 5);
(6) Daniel in the Lions' Den (Dan 6).

**Daniel 7-12 remain entirely excluded — no verse, scene description, or reference to their content
anywhere in this brief.** No item in this brief draws on Daniel 4:33 (the graphic physical
description of Nebuchadnezzar's madness), consistent with the medium brief's exclusion of that
specific verse from challenge-ready text.

ground for this tier** — every item above is either an exact reuse of an already-approved,
already-bounded verse (new blank only) or plain narrative-fact recall drawn from inside an

**Structural rules carried forward, confirmed to hold for this tier:**
- **Chapters 7-12 remain entirely excluded** — no verse, scene description, or content reference of
  any kind appears anywhere in this brief.

  passage, the same restrained register the medium brief uses — **not** from 4:33's graphic
  physical description, which remains excluded from challenge-ready text at every tier.

  fact — Daniel's own recounting of what Belshazzar did, consistent with the medium review's
  finding that this material carries no cross-tradition doctrinal dispute.

  review's finding.

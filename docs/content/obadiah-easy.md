## A note on this brief's scope and thinness

**This brief is intentionally thin, following the same 1 Chronicles/Obadiah-medium precedent this
book's medium brief names.** Obadiah is the shortest book in the Old Testament (21 verses, one
unified oracle), and its medium brief's 3 pivotal passages are each scoped to a small, specific
verse set (3-4; 10, 12; 15, 17) rather than a broader chapter range. **This brief deliberately does
not expand into verses the medium review never evaluated** (e.g. vv.1-2, 5-9, 11, 13-14, 16, 18-21)
— every item below is a new question built from the same 6 verses already theologically reviewed at
medium (Obadiah 3, 4, 10, 12, 15, 17), consistent with this assignment's "reuse the same pivotal
scenes/passages — no new scenes" instruction applied at its strictest for a book this narrow. Per
the medium brief's own precedent, this tier has **no distinct boss-battle structure** — a book this
short does not need one.

Translation: World English Bible (WEB) throughout.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
Obadiah's 3 pivotal passages and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. It does
**not** simplify or paraphrase verse text. Difficulty skews to rank 1 throughout.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/obadiah.md`, no new scenes added

Same 3: Edom's Pride Condemned (3-4); Edom's Offense Against Judah (10, 12); The Day of Yahweh and
Deliverance on Mount Zion (15, 17).

## Challenge Items (6, all multiple-choice, difficulty_rank 1)

1. **Obadiah 1:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "The pride of your heart has
   deceived you, you who dwell in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high, who says in his
   heart, 'Who will bring me down to the ground?'" — MC: "According to Obadiah 1:3, what has deceived
   Edom?" Options: the pride of your heart / the wealth of your land / the strength of your army /
   the wisdom of your elders. Correct: the pride of your heart.
2. **Obadiah 1:4** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Though you mount on high as
   the eagle, and though your nest is set among the stars, I will bring you down from there," says
   Yahweh." — MC: "According to Obadiah 1:4, where does the text say Edom's nest is set?" Options:
   among the stars / in the valley / on the sea / in the desert. Correct: among the stars.
3. **Obadiah 1:10** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "For the violence done to
   your brother Jacob, shame will cover you, and you will be cut off forever." — MC: "According to
   Obadiah 1:10, whose violence brings shame on Edom?" Options: your brother Jacob / a foreign king /
   your own people / the Assyrians. Correct: your brother Jacob.
4. **Obadiah 1:12** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "But don't look down on your
   brother in the day of his disaster, and don't rejoice over the children of Judah in the day of
   their destruction." — MC: "According to Obadiah 1:12, what does the text say Edom should not have
   done in the day of Judah's disaster?" Options: looked down on their brother / helped their brother
   / traded with their brother / warned their brother. Correct: looked down on their brother.
5. **Obadiah 1:15** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "For the day of Yahweh is
   near all the nations! As you have done, it will be done to you. Your deeds will return upon your
   own head." — MC: "According to Obadiah 1:15, what is 'near all the nations'?" Options: the day of
   Yahweh / a great famine / a new king / peace. Correct: the day of Yahweh.
6. **Obadiah 1:17** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "But in Mount Zion, there
   will be those who escape, and it will be holy. The house of Jacob will possess their
   possessions." — MC: "According to Obadiah 1:17, what does the text say will happen on Mount
   Zion?" Options: there will be those who escape, and it will be holy / a new temple will be built
   there / Edom will be defeated there / Elijah will appear there. Correct: there will be those who
   escape, and it will be holy.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/obadiah.md` applies unchanged, including the "Edom as a
later cipher for Rome/Christendom in Jewish tradition" bounding.

**New for this tier — reviewed in `docs/reviews/obadiah-easy-review.md`, bounding confirmed to
hold:**

- **All 6 items test only what Obadiah's own text states about the historical nation of Edom named
  in the oracle** — none asks what "Edom" symbolizes in later tradition or identifies Edom with any
  later or contemporary nation or group, carrying forward the medium brief's structural rule
  unchanged.
- **No item evaluates whether Edom's announced punishment was proportionate or just** (items 3-5 in
  particular stay strictly on narrative fact — what the text says happened/will happen — never a
  moral judgment on it).
- **Obadiah 1:18's most totalizing destruction language remains unused** in this brief, exactly as
  at medium.
- **This tier introduces no verse beyond the 6 already reviewed at medium** — no new cross-tradition
  research was required beyond re-confirming each existing bounding still applies to the new
  question format used here, which it does in every case.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi's "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge" direction. This tier draws on less
commonly memorized, secondary details from the SAME 4 scenes — place names, exact wording — and
requires typed recall wherever the format allows. As with the easy tier, this brief draws **only**
on verse text already quoted verbatim and reviewed in the medium brief. Every "less commonly
memorized" fact below (the river Kishon, the town of Bethel, Gideon's family being "the poorest in
Manasseh") is a secondary detail already sitting inside an already-approved multi-clause quotation —
this tier simply blanks a different word than the easy tier or medium brief did.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/judges.md`, no new scenes added

Same 4 scenes as `medium` and `easy`: The Cycle Begins (Judges 2:11-19), Deborah and Barak (Judges 4,
Jael excluded), Gideon and the Three Hundred (Judges 6-7), The Cycle Ends — For Now (Judges 21:25).

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/judges.md` applies unchanged. **Nothing new is
introduced by this brief**: no new verse, no new scene.

- **Jephthah's daughter (Judges 11) and the Levite's concubine (Judges 19) — confirmed absent.**
- **Jael's killing of Sisera (Judges 4:17-22, 5:24-27) — confirmed absent.** Items 4 and 11 (both
  drawn from Judges 4:6-16) stop at "I will deliver him into your hand" / "Yahweh has delivered
  Sisera into your hand" — the promise and the army's defeat, never Sisera's death, which happens
  several verses later in the excluded material.
- **Judges 4:4-5 (Deborah's role) format compliance.** Item 3 is recall/fill-in-blank, grades only
  the place name "Bethel," and does not ask what her example implies for any present-day leadership
  question. **Compliant.**
- **New structural guard — Judges 4:5, where Deborah lived. Added per review §6.7/Finding, required.**
  Cross-tradition check: classic Protestant commentary (Bible Hub, Judges 4:5) and the Catholic NABRE
  both print the same two place names, Ramah and Bethel, in the same order. Jewish tradition (Rashi on
  Judges 4:5, via Sefaria) reads the geography *non-literally* — the palm trees, vineyards, and olive
  trees are Deborah's *property holdings*, evidence of her wealth, and on this reading she actually
  resided in Ataroth; the verse's places are "not meant literally" as her dwelling. WEB, NABRE and the
  Jewish commentary tradition all print/assume the word "Bethel" in this verse — Rashi disputes what
  the list *means*, not what the text *says* — so the bounding holds and item 3 (which grades the
  printed word) is unaffected. **Structural rule: no challenge item in any tier may grade *where
  Deborah lived* as a narrative fact (e.g. "Where did Deborah live?" → "between Ramah and Bethel").
  Grading the word the verse prints, as item 3 does, remains fine.**
- **New structural guard — Judges 4:7, "river" Kishon. Added per review §6.8, recommended.** WEB reads
  "the river Kishon"; NABRE reads "the Wadi Kishon" — a descriptor difference, not a name difference.
  Item 4 grades only the proper name "Kishon," which is safe as written. **No item in any tier should
  grade the descriptor "river," only the name "Kishon."**
- **Judges 6:37 (Gideon's fleece test) format compliance.** Item 6 is recall/fill-in-blank, grades
  only the word "spoken," and does not ask whether the test was righteous or doubtful. **Compliant.**
  *(Source-availability note carried from the review: Sefaria returned empty commentary arrays for
  Rashi and Radak on this verse and a GotQuestions URL 404'd, so this run's cross-tradition check for
  6:37 rests on classic Protestant commentary alone rather than a fresh two-tradition confirmation. The
  bounding still holds because the divergence that exists is entirely about Gideon's motive, and the
  graded token "spoken" is untouched by it — recorded here per the review's request that this gap be
  weighed by a human reviewer rather than papered over.)*
- **No other new contested ground.** All eight regular items reuse clauses from verse blocks already
  verified character-for-character in `docs/reviews/judges-review.md` §1 — this brief only changes
  which word inside each already-approved quotation is blanked.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge," following the Genesis pilot's design
intent (`docs/content/genesis-hard.md`). This tier draws on less commonly memorized, more precise
clauses from within the same already-approved verse ranges, and requires typed recall (no
multiple-choice scaffolding) for every regular item. All items in this brief sit at
**`difficulty_rank` 5**. Same guardrails as every other tier: verbatim WEB text only, narrative
fact only, no doctrinal position required.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/ezekiel.md`, no new passages added

Same 6 passages, same verse ranges medium already scoped: (1) The Call Vision and Commissioning
(Ezek 1:1-3, 1:26-28, 2:1-10, 3:1-4); (2) The Sign-Act: Bearing Israel's and Judah's Iniquity
(Ezek 4:1-8); (3) The Watchman (Ezek 3:16-21; 33:1-11); (4) Individual Responsibility (Ezek 18:1-4,
20, 23); (5) The Shepherd of Israel (Ezek 34:1-6, 11-16, 23-24); (6) The Valley of Dry Bones
(Ezek 37:1-14).

**Every verse below is drawn from inside these same six already-approved ranges — none extends
into Ezekiel 1:4-25 (the wheel/creature imagery, deliberately not needed for a clean challenge item
at any tier), 2:5-8, 3:5-16/20-21, 4:2-3/4/6-8 beyond what's already used, 18:5-19/21-22/24-32,
33:1-3/7-10, 34:7-10/17-22, or 37:15 onward.** Ezekiel 16, 23, 38-39, and the temple vision
(40-48) remain entirely excluded, per the medium brief.

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

   rainbow that is in the cloud in the day of rain, so was the appearance of the brightness all

   **glory**.

   and ___ were written in it." → **woe**.

   this ___, and go, speak to the house of Israel.'" → **scroll**.
4. **Ezek 4:5** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "For I have appointed the years of their

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/ezekiel.md` applies unchanged. **No new contested
ground for this tier** — every item above is either an exact reuse of an already-approved,
already-bounded verse (new blank or format only) or plain narrative/descriptive recall drawn from
inside an already-scoped verse range.

**Structural rules carried forward, confirmed to hold for this tier:**
- **Ezekiel 16, 23, and 38-39 remain entirely excluded** — no verse, scene description, or content
  reference of any kind appears anywhere in this brief.
- **Ezekiel 34:23-24, "my servant David": recall/fill-in-blank format only, never multiple-choice.**
  Item 8 blanks "prince" rather than "David" (item 8 of `easy` blanks "David") — a different word
  from the same restricted verse, still never asking who the figure is or what he represents, and
  this entire tier's recall-by-default design makes the restriction naturally satisfied.
- **The 390-day count (Ezekiel 4:5):** item 4 tests only the text's own stated number, never its
  chronological referent or symbolic meaning — same bounding as the medium brief's Level 5 example
  and the easy tier's item 7 (which used the shepherd-condemnation fact from the same passage
  instead of the day-count).
- **Ezekiel 18 vs. Exodus 20:5:** item 6 tests only Ezekiel 18:23's own stated content, not the
  harmonization question.
- **Ezekiel 37 (dry bones):** items 9 and boss item 10 test only the text's own wording, never the
  rabbinic literal-vs-allegory dispute or the vision's meaning beyond what 18:20/3:17/37:4 state
  outright.
- **The temple vision (Ezekiel 40-48) remains out of scope** — no verse from these chapters
  appears.

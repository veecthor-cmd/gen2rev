## Sensitivity note (inherited, read before the items below)

Per `docs/content/2-kings.md`, this book's second half narrates two national military catastrophes.
This tier keeps the same posture: factual, not graphic. **Specifically excluded, same as `medium`:**
2 Kings 25:6-7 (Zedekiah's sons killed, his eyes put out), 2 Kings 6:26-29 (cannibalism during an
earlier siege), and 2 Kings 2:23-24 (children mauled by bears). 2 Kings 19:35 stays trimmed to the
bare fact (a number, and Sennacherib's withdrawal) with no elaboration on how the deaths occurred.

---

## Design intent for this tier

Draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from 2 Kings's 5 scenes and favors multiple-choice
recognition over typed recall, so a first-time player with no prior Bible knowledge can succeed. It
does not simplify or paraphrase verse text.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/2-kings.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes: Elijah's Ascension and Elisha's Succession (2 Kings 2:1-15), The Fall of the Northern
Kingdom (2 Kings 17:5-23), Hezekiah and the Assyrian Siege (2 Kings 18-19), Josiah's Reforms
(2 Kings 22-23), The Fall of Jerusalem and the Babylonian Exile (2 Kings 25).

## Challenge Items (16: 12 regular + 4 boss)

### Regular (12)

**Scene 1 — Elijah's Ascension and Elisha's Succession**

   I am taken from you.' Elisha said, 'Please let a double portion of your spirit be on me.'" *(Trim
   now ellipsis-marked per review §6.5 — the omitted opening, "When they had gone over, Elijah said

   "What did Elisha ask for before Elijah was taken from him?" Options: a double portion of his
   spirit / gold and silver / a place in heaven / his mantle. Correct: a double portion of his
   spirit.
2. **2 Kings 2:11** (WEB, already-approved verse): "As they continued on and talked, behold, a
   chariot of fire and horses of fire separated them; and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into
   heaven." — MC: "What appeared and separated Elijah from Elisha?" Options: a chariot of fire and
   horses of fire / a great wind / an earthquake / a bright cloud. Correct: a chariot of fire and
   horses of fire.
3. **2 Kings 2:15** (WEB, already-approved verse — *not actually trimmed; label corrected per review

   the other prophets say when they saw Elisha?" Options: "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha" /
   "Elijah has returned" / "Elisha is now king" / "The Lord has abandoned us." Correct: "The spirit
   of Elijah rests on Elisha."

**Scene 2 — The Fall of the Northern Kingdom**

   Assyria do to Samaria?" Options: Besieged it three years, then captured it and carried Israel away

   years, then captured it and carried Israel away.
5. **2 Kings 17:23** (WEB, already-approved verse, trimmed): "...Yahweh removed Israel out of his

**Scene 3 — Hezekiah and the Assyrian Siege**

   Assyria came up against all the fortified cities of Judah and took them." — MC: **"According to

   Sennacherib king of Assyria / Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon / Pharaoh king of Egypt / Cyrus king

8. **2 Kings 19:35-36** (WEB, already-approved verse, trimmed — no elaboration on the mechanism of

   to the Assyrian army that night?"** Options: Yahweh's angel struck them down, and Sennacherib

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/2-kings.md` applies unchanged: the 17:7-23 causation
question (17:7 itself is not selected in this tier), the 19:35 mass-casualty sensitivity handling
(item 8 stays trimmed to the bare fact, per the inherited rule), the 25:27-30 vs. 2 Chronicles
36:22-23 two-endings note, the 2 Kings 2 NT-typology exclusion, and the exclusions of 25:6-7,
6:26-29, and 2:23-24 (none selected in this tier).

**New for this tier — reviewed against live cross-tradition sources in
`docs/reviews/2-kings-easy-review.md` §4, bounding confirmed to hold in every case:**

- **2 Kings 18:13 (item 7)**: Sennacherib's Judean campaign is also referenced in Assyrian royal
  records (the Taylor Prism), and mainstream commentators (Barnes, Pulpit Commentary — review §4.2)
  find the "fourteenth year" note itself "hopelessly at variance with the Assyrian dates," even
  proposing it as a later marginal insertion. This item grades only what the text itself states —
  **who invaded** — never the date's historical reliability. *(Bounding sentence corrected per
  review §6.3 — the earlier draft's "who invaded, and when" wrongly implied the year itself was safe
  to grade; it is not.)* **Structural rule, added per review §6.3: the "fourteenth year" may be
  displayed as verse text but must never be the graded token or a multiple-choice subject in any
  tier.**
- **2 Kings 22:13 (item 10)**: Josiah's own stated reason for alarm ("Yahweh's wrath... kindled
  against us") sits in the same causation-adjacent territory as 17:7's already-bounded item. This
  item quotes Josiah's own words as direct in-text quotation; it does not extend to a general claim
  about divine wrath and national calamity.
- **2 Kings 2:14 (item 16)**: narrative description of the succession scene, inside the existing "no
  NT typology" bounding for 2 Kings 2. No new ground. *(Corrected per review §6.1 — an earlier draft
  of this bullet also listed "2:10, 2:12, 2:13 (hard tier)," copy-paste residue from a hard-tier
  draft; none of those verses is used anywhere in this brief.)*
- **2 Kings 2 — mantle and double portion, structural rule added per review §6.6**: Orthodox
  tradition reads the mantle's transfer as the transmission of prophetic authority, alongside a
  distinctively Orthodox Elijah/Enoch eschatology (OrthodoxWiki) — a genuine, live cross-tradition
  layer of meaning distinct from the plain narrative facts items 1 and 16 grade. **No challenge in
  any tier may ask what the mantle or the double portion *signifies, confers, or prefigures* — only
  what was asked for, taken, struck, and what happened.** Items 1 and 16 already comply; this makes
  compliance a stated rule rather than an accident.
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — items 1-6, 9, 11-12 reuse already-approved verse text
  under the same bounding already reviewed at `medium`.

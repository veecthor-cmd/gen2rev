## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi's "easy... real simple" design direction (established for the Genesis pilot). This tier
draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from Judges's 4 approved scenes and favors
multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. This brief draws **only** on verse text already
quoted verbatim and reviewed in the medium brief — zero new verses, zero incremental review risk
beyond format compliance with the two existing structural rules (Judges 4:4-5 and 6:37, both
recall-only, never multiple-choice).

## Challenge Items (11: 8 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (8)

1. **Judges 2:16** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Yahweh raised up judges, who saved
   them out of the hand of those who plundered them." — MC: "Who did Yahweh raise up to save Israel
   from those who plundered them?" Options: judges / kings / priests / prophets. Correct: judges.
2. **Judges 4:4-5** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Now Deborah, a prophetess, the wife
   of Lappidoth, judged Israel at that time. She lived under Deborah's palm tree between Ramah and
   Bethel in the hill country of Ephraim; and the children of Israel came up to her for judgment." —
   recall: "Now Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lappidoth, ___ Israel at that time." → **judged**.
   *(Recall format only — never multiple-choice — per the existing structural rule; see Contested
   Territory below.)*
3. **Judges 4:14-16** (WEB, trimmed, already-approved verse, new format): "Deborah said to Barak, 'Go;
   for this is the day in which Yahweh has delivered Sisera into your hand...' Yahweh confused Sisera,
   all his chariots, and all his army, with the edge of the sword before Barak... all the army of
   Sisera fell by the edge of the sword. There was not a man left." — MC: **"According to Judges 4:16,
   what happened to Sisera's army when Yahweh gave Barak the victory?"** *(Stem anchored per review
   §6.4, third bullet: makes explicit the item grades the army's fate, not Sisera's — the trim elides
   4:15's "Sisera abandoned his chariot and fled away on his feet," so a player should not infer he
   died with the army.)* Options: The whole army fell by the edge of the sword; not a man was left /
   They surrendered without a fight / They fled and were never seen again / Barak spared them all.
   Correct: The whole army fell by the edge of the sword; not a man was left. *(Stops exactly
   where the medium brief's scene description stops — the army's defeat, never Sisera's own death by
   Jael, which is excluded; see Contested Territory below.)*
4. **Judges 6:14-16** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Yahweh looked at him, and said, 'Go
   in this your might, and save Israel from the hand of Midian. Haven't I sent you?' He said to him,
   'O Lord, how shall I save Israel? Behold, my family is the poorest in Manasseh, and I am the least
   in my father's house.' Yahweh said to him, 'Surely I will be with you, and you shall strike the
   Midianites as one man.'" — MC: "What did Yahweh tell Gideon to do?" Options: Go in his might and
   save Israel from Midian / Build an altar before doing anything else / Gather ten thousand men
   immediately / Flee to Egypt. Correct: Go in his might and save Israel from Midian.
5. **Judges 6:37** (WEB, trimmed, already-approved verse, new format; *label per review §6.6*):
   "...behold, I will put a fleece of wool on the threshing floor. If there is dew on the fleece only,
   and it is dry on all the ground, then I'll know that you will save Israel by my hand, as you have
   spoken." — recall: "...behold, I will put a fleece of ___ on the threshing floor." → **wool**.
   *(Recall format only — never multiple choice — per the existing structural rule; see Contested
   Territory below.)*
6. **Judges 7:2** (WEB, trimmed, already-approved verse, new format; *label per review §6.6*): "The
   people who are with you are too many for me to give the Midianites into their hand, lest Israel
   brag against me, saying, 'My own hand has saved me.'" — MC: "Why did Yahweh say Gideon's army was
   too large?" Options: So that Israel could not boast the victory was by their own hand / Because
   there wasn't enough food for them / Because the men were afraid / Because Midian had a much bigger
   army. Correct: so Israel couldn't claim credit for the victory.
7. **Judges 7:20-21** (WEB, trimmed, already-approved verse, new format; *label per review §6.6*):
   "...they shouted, 'The sword of Yahweh and of Gideon!' They each stood in his place around the
   camp, and all the army ran; and they shouted, and put them to flight." — MC: **"According to Judges
   7:20, what did Gideon's three hundred men shout during the attack on the Midianite camp?"** *(Stem
   anchored per review §6.4, second bullet: Judges 7:18 records Gideon *instructing* a differently-
   worded shout, "For Yahweh and for Gideon!," while 7:20 records what was actually shouted; no current
   distractor collides with 7:18, but anchoring makes the answer key safe permanently.)* Options: 'The
   sword of Yahweh and of Gideon!' / 'Long live the king!' / 'Peace be with you' / They shouted
   nothing. Correct: The sword of Yahweh and of Gideon.
8. **Judges 21:25** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "In those days there was no king in
   Israel. Everyone did that which was right in his own eyes." — MC: **"According to Judges 21:25,
   what was true of Israel in those days?"** *(Fix per review §6.2, required: the stem is reframed
   from causal "why" to narrative fact. The original stem — "why did everyone do what was right in
   their own eyes?" — graded an inference the text does not assert as a causal link; the Hebrew
   juxtaposes the two clauses with no causal connective, and mainstream commentary is not unanimous
   about what "no king" is doing in the sentence. The reworded stem grades only what the verse states
   outright.)* Options: There was no king in Israel, and everyone did what was right in his own eyes /
   The judges had all died out / The land was completely at peace / The people had forgotten the law.
   Correct: There was no king in Israel, and everyone did what was right in his own eyes.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/judges.md` applies unchanged. **Nothing new is
introduced by this brief**: no new verse, no new scene.

- **Jephthah's daughter (Judges 11) and the Levite's concubine (Judges 19) — confirmed absent.**
  Neither chapter, nor any reference to either episode, appears anywhere in this brief.
- **Jael's killing of Sisera (Judges 4:17-22, 5:24-27) — confirmed absent.** Item 3 (4:14-16) stops
  at "the whole army fell by the edge of the sword; not a man left," exactly where the medium brief's
  own scene description and challenge-ready verse selection stop. No item references the tent-peg
  killing.
- **Judges 4:4-5 (Deborah's role) format compliance.** The existing structural rule restricts this
  verse to recall/fill-in-blank or narrative-sequence format only, never multiple-choice. Item 2 is
  recall/fill-in-blank, grades only the word "judged," and does not ask what her example implies for
  any present-day leadership question. **Compliant.**
- **Judges 6:37 (Gideon's fleece test) format compliance.** The existing structural rule restricts
  this verse to recall/fill-in-blank format only, never multiple-choice. Item 5 is recall/fill-in-
  blank, grades only the word "wool," and does not ask whether the test was righteous or doubtful.
  **Compliant.**
- **"The angel of Yahweh" (Judges 6:11-12) — new bullet, added per review §6.3.** Item 4 quotes 6:14-16,
  where the speaker is named "Yahweh," but three verses earlier (6:11-12), inside the same approved
  scene, the speaker is "the angel of Yahweh." Who that figure is, is a genuine cross-tradition
  question: the Geneva Study Bible and Gill identify the speaker as a theophany/pre-incarnate Christ or
  "Jehovah himself," while Ellicott offers the alternative that "the angel only speaks as the mouth of
  God" and explicitly leaves both readings open (Bible Hub, Judges 6:14 commentaries). Item 4's graded
  answer is the content of Yahweh's command, which every reading renders identically — the item does
  not require a player to affirm who the angel is. **Structural rule: no challenge item in any Judges
  tier may ask who appeared or spoke to Gideon at the oak (Judges 6:11-12), or grade the identity of
  "the angel of Yahweh." Grading the content of the command in 6:14-16, as item 4 does, remains fine.**
- **"The wife of Lappidoth" (Judges 4:4-5) — new forward guard, added per review §6.4.** Item 2
  displays "the wife of Lappidoth" but grades only "judged," so nothing is currently wrong — but Rashi
  on Judges 4:4 reads *eshet Lappidot* non-onomastically ("she fashioned wicks for the sanctuary"),
  i.e. not as a husband's proper name at all, while WEB, NABRE and JPS all treat it as a name.
  **Structural rule: "wife of Lappidoth" is display-only and is never a graded token, and no challenge
  at any tier may ask "who was Deborah's husband?"**
- **No other new contested ground.** Every item above draws on verse text already verified in
  `docs/reviews/judges-review.md` §1; no new quotation or narrative claim beyond what that review
  already checked.

## Design intent for this tier, and a deliberate scope decision specific to this book

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall, so
a first-time or younger player can succeed without prior Bible knowledge — same as every other book's
`easy` tier.

**Unlike every other book's easy/hard tier in this project so far (including this book's own sibling,
Ecclesiastes), this brief introduces no new verse content whatsoever.** It reuses only the 4 passages
and exactly the same verses already named in `docs/content/song-of-solomon.md` — the refrain (2:7,
3:5, 8:4), the voice of spring (2:10-13), mutual belonging (2:16a, trimmed), and love strong as death
(8:6-7) — varying only the question format and which word or clause is graded. This is a deliberate,
conservative choice, not an oversight: this book's `medium` brief was escalated to a human reviewer
specifically because its whole-book framing and general-audience distribution risk could not be fully
resolved by narrow verse-level bounding alone (`docs/reviews/song-of-solomon-review.md` §3.1, §3.6),
and Kachi's resolution explicitly accepted "the four passages exactly as drafted" with "no loosening
of the brief's existing exclusions." Pulling additional verses from elsewhere in the book — even from
within the same four chapters, even material that reads as innocuous in isolation — would reopen a
question Kachi already closed, so this tier does not do that. Every exclusion named in the `medium`
brief's Contested Territory section (chapters 1:2, 4, 6, 7; the embrace verses 2:6/3:4/8:3; "he
browses among the lilies," 2:16b) carries forward here unchanged, with no exception.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/song-of-solomon.md`, no new passages, no new verses

Same 4 passages, same verses exactly:

1. The Refrain — Song of Solomon 2:7, 3:5, 8:4
2. The Voice of Spring — Song of Solomon 2:10-13

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Song 2:7** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "I adjure you, daughters of
   Jerusalem, by the roes, or by the hinds of the field, that you not stir up, nor awaken love,
   until it so desires." — MC: "According to Song of Solomon 2:7, what does the speaker adjure the
   daughters of Jerusalem not to do?" Options: stir up or awaken love until it desires / build a
   house / plant a vineyard / go to war. Correct: stir up or awaken love until it desires.
2. **Song 3:5 / 2:7 / 8:4** (WEB, already-approved verses, new structural-fact question): the same
   adjuration appears at 2:7, 3:5, and 8:4. — MC: "How many times does the adjuration 'do not stir up
   nor awaken love until it so desires' appear in the book?" Options: three times / once / twice /
   four times. Correct: three times.
3. **Song 8:4** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "I adjure you, daughters of
   Jerusalem, that you not stir up, nor awaken love, until it so desires." — MC: "Song of Solomon
   8:4 repeats the refrain — to whom is it addressed?" Options: the daughters of Jerusalem / the sons
   of Israel / the elders of the city / the shepherds. Correct: the daughters of Jerusalem.
4. **Song 2:10-13** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "...For behold, the winter is
   past. The rain is over and gone..." — MC: "According to Song of Solomon 2:10-13, what has passed,
   in the invitation to 'rise up, my love'?" Options: the winter / the summer / the harvest / the
   famine. Correct: the winter.
5. **Song 2:10-13** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle, different clause than item 4):
   "...The time of the singing has come, and the voice of the turtledove is heard in our land..." —
   MC: "According to Song of Solomon 2:10-13, whose voice is heard in the land?" Options: the
   turtledove / the nightingale / the lamb / the shepherd. Correct: the turtledove.
6. **Song 2:16a** (WEB, already-approved verse, trimmed per `medium`, new question angle): "My
   beloved is mine, and I am his." — MC: "According to Song of Solomon 2:16, what does the speaker
   declare?" Options: "My beloved is mine, and I am his." / "I am my beloved's forever." / "My
   beloved is a shepherd." / "I am the rose of Sharon." Correct: "My beloved is mine, and I am his."
7. **Song 8:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "...for love is strong as death..."
   — MC: "According to Song of Solomon 8:6, love is said to be as strong as what?" Options: death /
   fire / the sea / a lion. Correct: death.

### Boss (3)

8. **Song 8:7** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Many waters can't quench love,
   neither can floods drown it..." — MC: "According to Song of Solomon 8:7, what can't quench love?"
   Options: many waters / much wisdom / great wealth / strong wind. Correct: many waters.
9. **Boss sequence — genuinely new item, ordering only**: place the book's 4 pivotal passages in the
   order they are first encountered in the book — The Refrain, first at 2:7 → The Voice of Spring,
   2:10-13 → Mutual Belonging, 2:16 → Love Strong as Death, 8:6-7.
10. **Song 2:7 / 3:5 / 8:4** (WEB, already-approved verses, new structural-fact question): — MC:
    "Which daughters are addressed in the refrain repeated at 2:7, 3:5, and 8:4?" Options: the
    daughters of Jerusalem / the daughters of Zion / the daughters of Israel / the daughters of
    Solomon. Correct: the daughters of Jerusalem.

## Contested Territory

**Everything already flagged in `docs/content/song-of-solomon.md` applies unchanged, without
exception, per this tier's design intent above:**

- **Whole-book interpretive framing (allegorical vs. literal).** Still a live, unresolved,
  centuries-old cross-tradition question this brief takes no position on. No item, flavor text, or
  world description in this tier asserts either reading as the book's "real" meaning.
- **Deliberately excluded, unchanged: the body-description poems (Song of Solomon 4, 6, 7) and the
  opening "kisses of his mouth" (1:2).** No verse from these chapters appears anywhere in this brief.
- **The refrain's immediate context (2:6, 3:4, 8:3).** Not used — challenge-ready text starts at
  2:7/3:5/8:4, exactly as in `medium`.
- **Song of Solomon 2:16's second clause, "He browses among the lilies."** Still excluded; item 6
  trims to the same sentence boundary as `medium`.
- **Song of Solomon 8:6's "a very flame of Yah."** Not used in this tier's items (item 7's blank is
  "death," not this clause) — the display text quoted in item 7 stops before this clause, so the
  question does not engage it at all.
- **General-audience / child-safety distribution risk.** Already escalated and resolved by Kachi at
  `medium` (ship as scoped). This tier adds no new material and no new risk surface — see design
  intent above for why that is a deliberate choice, not an assumption.

**No new contested territory was introduced by this tier**, because no new verse content was
introduced. This is confirmed, not merely asserted, in `docs/reviews/song-of-solomon-easy-review.md`.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge," following the Genesis pilot's design
intent (`docs/content/genesis-hard.md`). This tier draws on less commonly memorized, more precise
clauses from within the same already-approved verse ranges, and requires typed recall (no
multiple-choice scaffolding) for every regular item. All items in this brief sit at
**`difficulty_rank` 5**. Same guardrails as every other tier: verbatim WEB text only, narrative
fact only, no doctrinal position required.

## Scenes/Passages — reused from `docs/content/job.md`, no new scenes or passages added

**Narrative Frame Scenes (3):** (1) Job's Prosperity and the Test Begins (Job 1:1-3, 1:6-12);
(2) Job's Losses and His Response (Job 1:13-22, 2:7-10); (3) Job's Response and Restoration

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/job.md` applies unchanged: the core assignment
guardrail (no item may ask a player to affirm or reject a position on why God allows suffering, or
whether Job's friends' theology was right or wrong); "The Satan" figure (Job 1:6-12); Job 1:21;
Job 19:25-27; Job 42:6; Job 42:7; and all deliberately excluded material. **No new contested ground
for this tier** — every new verse (1:9-10, 2:7, 38:33, 39:25, 42:3) is plain narrative description
or God's-speech imagery already covered by the medium brief's analysis.

**Structural rules carried forward, confirmed to hold for this tier:**
- **"The Satan" figure (Job 1:6-12): items must be recall/fill-in-blank or narrative-fact multiple
  choice about actions/dialogue only — never a question asking who/what Satan is.** Item 2 (1:9-10)
  is a verbatim recall of Satan's own words, not an identity question — compliant, and this tier's
  recall-only default makes the distinction moot in practice.
- **Job 1:21, Job 19:25-27, and Job 42:6 remain recall/fill-in-blank format only, never
  multiple-choice.** Items 3, 8, and boss item 10 (which quotes but does not grade a
  multiple-choice interpretation of 42:6) all honor this; this entire tier is recall-by-default, so
  the restriction is naturally satisfied.
- **Job 42:7 (God's rebuke of the friends) is not used as a standalone graded item in this brief** —
  it appears only as a narrative-summary label in the boss sequence (item 9), never a "why" question,
  consistent with the medium brief's framing.
- **Job 3-37, Job 40:15-41:34 (Behemoth/Leviathan), and Job 1:18-19 remain fully excluded — no
  verse from any of these ranges appears above**, including in the boss sequence's narrative labels.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
each of Nehemiah's 5 scenes and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall, so a
first-time or younger player can succeed without prior Bible knowledge. It does **not** simplify or
paraphrase verse text — the style guide's "verbatim WEB text only" rule applies identically at
every tier.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/nehemiah.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes as `medium`: (1) Nehemiah's Prayer and Request to the King (Nehemiah 1-2); (2)
Rebuilding the Wall Amid Opposition (Nehemiah 2-6); (3) Ezra Reads the Law at the Water Gate
(Nehemiah 8); (4) National Confession and Covenant Renewal (Nehemiah 9-10); (5) Nehemiah's Reforms
(Nehemiah 13). **Both structural exclusions from `medium` carry forward unchanged: Nehemiah
13:23-27 (and its 13:25 violent clause specifically) stays excluded from both challenge-ready
content and the Scene 5 description at every tier, and Nehemiah 9:4-37's historical-review prayer
stays unselected to avoid re-surfacing Joshua's already-bounded ḥerem material.** No item below
draws on any excluded verse or the unselected prayer content.

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Nehemiah 1:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "The remnant who are left of
   the captivity there in the province are in great affliction and reproach. The wall of Jerusalem
   is also broken down, and its gates are burned with fire." — MC: "According to Nehemiah 1:3, what
   had happened to Jerusalem's wall and gates?" Options: the wall was broken down and the gates
   burned with fire / the wall was rebuilt and the gates repaired / the wall was never built / the
   gates were opened to all nations. Correct: the wall was broken down and the gates burned with
   fire.
2. **Nehemiah 2:5** (WEB, already-approved verse [within 2:4-5], new question angle): "I said to the
   king, 'If it pleases the king, and if your servant has found favor in your sight, I ask that you
   would send me to Judah, to the city of my fathers' tombs, that I may build it.'" — MC: "According
   to Nehemiah 2:5, what did Nehemiah ask the king to let him do?" Options: go to Judah to rebuild
   the city of his fathers' tombs / stay in Susa as cupbearer / build a new palace / lead the king's
   army. Correct: go to Judah to rebuild the city of his fathers' tombs.
3. **Nehemiah 2:18** (WEB, already-approved verse [within 2:17-18], new question angle): "I told them
   about the hand of my God which was good on me, and also about the king's words that he had spoken
   to me. They said, 'Let's rise up and build.' So they strengthened their hands for the good work."
   — MC: "According to Nehemiah 2:18, what did the people say after hearing about God's good hand on
   Nehemiah?" Options: "Let's rise up and build" / "We refuse to help" / "Let's ask the king first" /
   "We are too afraid." Correct: "Let's rise up and build."
4. **Nehemiah 4:17** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Those who built the wall,
   and those who bore burdens loaded themselves; everyone with one of his hands did the work, and
   with the other held his weapon." — MC: "According to Nehemiah 4:17, how did the builders work?"
   Options: with one hand doing the work and the other holding a weapon / only at night / without
   any tools / in complete silence. Correct: with one hand doing the work and the other holding a
   weapon.
5. **Nehemiah 8:10** (WEB, already-approved verse [within 8:9-10], new question angle, different
   clause than `medium`'s quoted trim): "Then he said to them, 'Go your way. Eat the fat, drink the
   sweet, and send portions to him for whom nothing is prepared, for today is holy to our Lord.
   Don't be grieved, for the joy of Yahweh is your strength.'" — MC: "According to Nehemiah 8:10,
   what were the people told to do since the day was holy?" Options: eat the fat, drink the sweet,
   and send portions to those with nothing prepared / fast completely / weep and mourn / work extra
   hard. Correct: eat the fat, drink the sweet, and send portions to those with nothing prepared.
6. **Nehemiah 8:17** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle; verbatim-QA restored the
   verse's final sentence, silently dropped in the prior draft pull): "All the assembly of those
   who had come back out of the captivity made temporary shelters and lived in the temporary
   shelters, for since the days of Joshua the son of Nun to that day the children of Israel had not
   done so. There was very great gladness." — MC: "According to Nehemiah 8:17, how long had it been since the Feast of Booths was
   kept so fully?" Options: since the days of Joshua son of Nun / since the days of Moses / since the
   days of David / never before at all. Correct: since the days of Joshua son of Nun.
7. **Nehemiah 13:19** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "It came to pass that when
   the gates of Jerusalem began to be dark before the Sabbath, I commanded that the doors should be
   shut, and commanded that they should not be opened until after the Sabbath..." — MC: "According to
   Nehemiah 13:19, what did Nehemiah command to protect the Sabbath?" Options: that the city gates be
   shut before the Sabbath began / that the markets stay open longer / that the priests leave the
   city / that the wall be torn down. Correct: that the city gates be shut before the Sabbath began.
   *(Sabbath-reform content only, within Scene 5's approved bounding — never touches 13:23-27; see

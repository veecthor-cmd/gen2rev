## Design intent for this tier, and the same deliberate scope decision as `easy`

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge." This tier requires typed recall (no
multiple-choice scaffolding) of less commonly memorized clauses, wherever the format allows.

**Like this book's `easy` tier, this brief introduces no new verse content whatsoever** — same 4
passages, same verses named in `docs/content/song-of-solomon.md`, varying only which clause is
graded and requiring the player to type it rather than recognize it. The reasoning is identical to
`easy`'s: Kachi's 2026-08-25 resolution of this book's escalation accepted "the four passages exactly
as drafted" with "no loosening of the brief's existing exclusions," and this tier does not reopen
that by reaching for additional material elsewhere in the book — even material within the same four
chapters that reads as harmless on its own. Every exclusion named in the `medium` brief carries
forward here unchanged.

## Contested Territory

**Everything already flagged in `docs/content/song-of-solomon.md` applies unchanged, without
exception, per this tier's design intent above** — whole-book interpretive framing, the excluded
chapters (1:2, 4, 6, 7), the excluded embrace verses (2:6/3:4/8:3), the excluded "he browses among the
lilies" clause (2:16b), and the general-audience distribution risk already resolved by Kachi.

**One item carries forward an existing bounding that this tier is the first to actually exercise as a
graded token, so it is restated explicitly, not silently inherited:**

- **Song of Solomon 8:6's "a very flame of Yah" (item 8).** The `medium` review (§3.4) already
  identified this as a genuine scholarly dispute — whether the Hebrew *shalhebetyah* contains a
  truncated form of the divine name (which would make this the book's only reference to God) or is
  simply an intensifying superlative — and ruled that any challenge built from this verse "tests
  recall of the verbatim WEB text only... and never asks what 'Yah' refers to." This brief's item 8
  is the first item in this book's content (across all three tiers) to actually make "Yah" the graded
  answer rather than just display it. **Structural rule, carried forward unchanged and now made
  binding on the graded token itself: this item is recall-only, never multiple-choice; no distractor
  option or question stem may ask what "Yah" refers to or whether this verse names God.**
- **Song of Solomon 8:6's "Sheol" (item 7).** Not previously graded at any tier. "Sheol" is the plain
  WEB text's own word for the realm of the dead — the same word appears without theological
  elaboration elsewhere in the OT canon already shipped (e.g. Ecclesiastes, Job, Psalms). No
  cross-tradition dispute was found specific to its use in this verse; the item grades only that the
  text says "Sheol," not what Sheol is.

**No new contested territory was introduced by this tier**, because no new verse content was
introduced. This is confirmed, not merely asserted, in `docs/reviews/song-of-solomon-hard-review.md`.

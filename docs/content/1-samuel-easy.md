## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi's "easy... real simple" framing (see `docs/content/genesis-easy.md`). This tier draws the
most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from each of 1 Samuel's 5 scenes and favors multiple-choice
recognition over typed recall, so a first-time or younger player can succeed without prior Bible
knowledge. It does not simplify or paraphrase verse text — the style guide's "verbatim WEB text
only" rule applies identically at every tier. **Distractor convention (added per review §6.3):**
invented MC distractors must not be near-renderings of any verse in the passage in play, and must
not reproduce WEB text belonging to a different speaker in that passage.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/1-samuel.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes: Samuel's Call (1 Sam 3), Israel Demands a King; Saul Anointed (1 Sam 8, 10), Saul's
Disobedience and Rejection; David Anointed (1 Sam 15, 16), David and Goliath (1 Sam 17), David and
Jonathan's Covenant; Saul's Pursuit of David (1 Sam 18, 24). **No item below draws on 1 Samuel
15:2-3 (the Amalek/ḥerem command) or any other verse from chapter 15** — that exclusion, already
established in `docs/content/1-samuel.md`, is carried forward unchanged; this tier does not even
reuse 15:22 (the one verse from that chapter medium does use), to keep this tier's footprint in
chapter 15 at zero.

## Challenge Items (11: 8 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (8)

1. **1 Sam 3:4-5** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Yahweh called Samuel. He said, 'Here
   I am.' He ran to Eli and said, 'Here I am; for you called me.' He said, 'I didn't call. Lie down
   again.' He went and lay down." — MC: "What did Samuel say when he ran to Eli, thinking Eli had
   called him?" Options: "Here I am; for you called me." / "I heard something and came to see what
   you needed." / "Why do you call me, father?" / "I am not worthy." Correct: "Here I am; for you
   called me." *(Distractor "Speak, Lord, I am listening." replaced per review Finding C [required]
   — it is a recognizable other-translation rendering of this passage's own famous line, so it read
   as a right answer from a different Bible offered to a first-time player as wrong, not a neutral
   invention.)*
2. **1 Sam 3:10** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Yahweh came, and stood, and called as
   at other times, 'Samuel! Samuel!' Then Samuel said, 'Speak; for your servant hears.'" — MC:
   "According to 1 Samuel 3:10, what did Samuel say when Yahweh came, stood, and called him?"
   Options: "Speak; for your servant hears." / "I don't understand, Lord." / "I am ready now, Lord."
   / "Who is calling me?" Correct: "Speak; for your servant hears." *(Stem re-anchored to the verse
   reference per review Finding A [required, blocking] — the original stem, "What did Samuel say the
   third time Yahweh called him?", is factually contradicted by 1 Sam 3:8 ("Yahweh called Samuel
   again the third time"), which makes the 3:10 call the fourth; the textually-correct answer to the
   original stem was sitting in the distractor list. Two distractors also replaced per Findings B and
   C [both required]: "Here I am; for you called me." was item 1's own correct answer (a cross-item
   collision), and "Go, lie down." was verbatim WEB text of 1 Sam 3:9, spoken by Eli, used as a wrong
   answer for Samuel — both defeat a beginner-tier item's purpose even though neither is scripturally
   "wrong" in the ordinary sense.)*
3. **1 Sam 8:5** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "They said to him, 'Behold, you are
   old, and your sons don't walk in your ways. Now make us a king to judge us like all the
   nations.'" — MC: "What did the elders of Israel ask Samuel for?" Options: a king / more judges /
   a new prophet / a temple. Correct: a king.
4. **1 Sam 8:7** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Yahweh said to Samuel, 'Listen to the
   voice of the people in all that they tell you; for they have not rejected you, but they have
   rejected me as the king over them.'" — MC: **"According to 1 Samuel 8:7, whom did Yahweh say the
   people had rejected as their king?"** Options: Yahweh / Samuel / Eli / Saul. Correct: Yahweh.
   *(Stem anchored to the verse and grades only the narrated statement — see Contested Territory
   below; this extends the same "kingship request" bounding already reviewed for 8:5.)*
5. **1 Sam 10:1** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Then Samuel took the vial of oil and
   poured it on his head, then kissed him and said, 'Hasn't Yahweh anointed you to be prince over
   his inheritance?'" — MC: "What did Samuel do to Saul when he anointed him?" Options: poured oil
   on his head and kissed him / crowned him with gold / gave him a sword / built him a throne.
   Correct: poured oil on his head and kissed him.
6. **1 Sam 16:13** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Then Samuel took the horn of oil and
   anointed him in the middle of his brothers. Then Yahweh's Spirit came mightily on David from
   that day forward. So Samuel rose up and went to Ramah." — MC: "Who anointed David with the horn
   of oil, in the middle of his brothers?" Options: Samuel / Eli / Saul / Jonathan. Correct: Samuel.
7. **1 Sam 17:45** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Then David said to the Philistine,
   'You come to me with a sword, with a spear, and with a javelin; but I come to you in the name of
   Yahweh of Armies, the God of the armies of Israel, whom you have defied.'" — MC: "According to
   1 Samuel 17:45, in whose name did David say he came against Goliath?" Options: Yahweh of Armies /
   King Saul / his father Jesse / the prophet Samuel. Correct: Yahweh of Armies.
8. **1 Sam 24:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "He said to his men, 'Yahweh forbid
   that I should do this thing to my lord, Yahweh's anointed, to stretch out my hand against him,
   since he is Yahweh's anointed.'" — MC: "What did David call Saul, explaining to his men why he
   wouldn't harm him?" Options: Yahweh's anointed / an enemy / a false king / a friend. Correct:
   Yahweh's anointed.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/1-samuel.md` applies unchanged, including the exclusion
of 1 Samuel 15:2-3 (the Amalek/ḥerem command — not touched anywhere in this tier, not even via the
already-approved 15:22), the 1 Samuel 8 kingship-request bounding, the 1 Samuel 16:7
election/foreknowledge bounding, the 1 Samuel 18:1/18:3 recall-only structural rule, and the
1 Samuel 24 moral-judgment bounding.

**New for this tier — reviewed against the same bounding standard applied in
`docs/reviews/1-samuel-review.md`, flagged explicitly rather than silently assumed safe:**

- **1 Sam 8:7** (item 4) is a new selection from the already-flagged "Israel demands a king"
  contested ground (`docs/content/1-samuel.md` names 8:5 and Yahweh's statement to Samuel as
  in-bounds; 8:7 is that same statement, the next verse of it). The item grades only *whom Yahweh
  named as rejected* ("Yahweh") — never whether the people's request was itself sinful,
  Yahweh's accommodation reading, or which side of that live cross-tradition divergence (documented
  in §3 of `docs/reviews/1-samuel-review.md`, citing Ligonier vs. Enduring Word) is correct. This is
  the same narrative-only bounding already reviewed for 8:5; no new dispute is introduced by using
  the adjacent verse.
- **1 Sam 18:3** (item 11) is used in recall/fill-in-blank format only, per the existing structural
  rule ("1 Samuel 18:1 and 18:3 may only be used in recall/fill-in-blank format... never as a
  multiple-choice item asking players to characterize what kind of relationship or love this was" —
  `docs/content/1-samuel.md`). Confirmed compliant: this item is recall-only, blanks the word
  "covenant" (not "soul" or "loved"), and does not ask what kind of relationship this was.
  **Extended per review Finding H [recommended]:** the blank in any 18:3 recall item stays on
  "covenant"; the pronoun "he," and the words "loved" and "soul," are never the blanked token — the
  Hebrew is genuinely ambiguous as to *who* loved *whom* (Revised JPS supplies "[Jonathan]"
  editorially where WEB leaves it open), so blanking the pronoun would ask a player to resolve a
  translation decision, not recall a word. Item 11 already complies; this guards a later pass.
- **1 Sam 10:1** (item 5). **Added per review Finding F [recommended].** WEB/MT ends this verse at
  "...prince over his inheritance?"; the LXX/Vulgate tradition (and the Catholic NABRE) carries a
  substantially longer reading that continues with "You are the one who will govern... and save
  them from the power of their enemies," and Revised JPS footnotes the same variant while printing
  the shorter MT text. **Structural rule: no challenge in any tier may grade the content of what
  Samuel said at 10:1, or where the verse ends.** Grading the *act* — pouring oil on the head and
  kissing — is fine and is what item 5 does; every textual tradition attests it identically.
- **1 Samuel 24 versification.** **Added per review Finding G [recommended].** Hebrew/Jewish
  versification runs one verse ahead of WEB/KJV through this chapter (Sefaria carries item 8's verse
  at 24:7, not 24:6). Item 8 is unaffected because its stem cites no reference. Noted so that any
  future *anchored* stem on this chapter ("According to 1 Samuel 24:6...") is written with awareness
  that a Jewish-tradition reader locates the same words one verse earlier — harmless for grading,
  worth knowing before it surfaces in a Memory Vault citation or a shared-result screen.
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — 3:4-5, 3:10, 8:5, 16:13, 17:45, and 24:6 are all plain
  narrative description or in-text direct quotation with no live cross-tradition disagreement found,
  consistent with the theological reviewer's existing findings on these same verses/scenes.

**Not applied in this pass:** the review's Finding I (recommended) asks that the wording of
`docs/content/1-samuel.md`'s inherited 18:1/18:3 recall-only rule be clarified so it no longer
technically forbids the sequencing beat in boss item 10 (a contradiction between the rule's letter
and its purpose, which the reviewer judged satisfied here on the *purpose* reading). That edit
targets the already-approved medium-tier file, which is out of scope for this pass; flagged here as
an open follow-up rather than silently applied or silently dropped.

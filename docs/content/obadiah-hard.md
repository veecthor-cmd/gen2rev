## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge." This tier draws on precise, less-obvious
phrasing within Obadiah's 3 pivotal passages and requires typed recall throughout (no
multiple-choice scaffolding anywhere in this brief). Difficulty skews to rank 5 throughout.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
Joel's 3 pivotal passages and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. It does **not**
simplify or paraphrase verse text. Difficulty skews to rank 1 throughout. Given Joel's short length
(3 chapters), this brief stays proportionately compact — see item count below.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/joel.md`, no new scenes added

Same 3: The Locust Plague and the Call to Lament (1:2-4, 1:13-15); "Turn to Me with All Your Heart"
(2:12-13); "I Will Pour Out My Spirit on All Flesh" (2:28-29).

## Challenge Items (8: 6 regular + 2 boss)

### Regular (6, all multiple-choice, difficulty_rank 1)

1. **Joel 1:4** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "What the swarming locust has
   left, the great locust has eaten. What the great locust has left, the grasshopper has eaten. What
   the grasshopper has left, the caterpillar has eaten." — MC: "According to Joel 1:4, what ate what
   the great locust left behind?" Options: the grasshopper / a bird / a fish / nothing. Correct: the
   grasshopper.
2. **Joel 1:14** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Sanctify a fast. Call a solemn
   assembly. Gather the elders and all the inhabitants of the land to the house of Yahweh, your God,
   and cry to Yahweh." — MC: "According to Joel 1:14, where does Joel tell the people to gather?"
   Options: the house of Yahweh / the city gate / the mountain / the marketplace. Correct: the house
   of Yahweh.
3. **Joel 1:15** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle, iconic): "Alas for the day! For
   the day of Yahweh is at hand, and it will come as destruction from the Almighty." — MC: "According
   to Joel 1:15, what does Joel say is 'at hand'?" Options: the day of Yahweh / a great harvest / a
   new king / a famine. Correct: the day of Yahweh.
4. **Joel 2:13** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle, iconic): "Tear your heart and not
   your garments, and turn to Yahweh, your God; for he is gracious and merciful, slow to anger, and
   abundant in loving kindness, and relents from sending calamity." — MC: "According to Joel 2:13,
   what does the text say Yahweh is?" Options: gracious and merciful, slow to anger / just and severe
   / distant and silent / swift to anger. Correct: gracious and merciful, slow to anger.
5. **Joel 2:28** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle, the book's most iconic line): "It
   will happen afterward, that I will pour out my Spirit on all flesh; and your sons and your
   daughters will prophesy. Your old men will dream dreams. Your young men will see visions." — MC:
   "According to Joel 2:28, on whom will Yahweh pour out his Spirit?" Options: all flesh / only the
   priests / only the prophets / only the king. Correct: all flesh. *(Tests only the text's own
   stated scope — never timing or fulfillment; see Contested Territory below.)*
6. **Joel 2:29** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "And also on the servants and on
   the handmaids in those days, I will pour out my Spirit." — MC: "According to Joel 2:29, besides
   sons, daughters, old men, and young men, who else does the text name?" Options: the servants and
   handmaids / the elders and priests / the farmers and vinedressers / the soldiers. Correct: the
   servants and handmaids.

### Boss (2)

8. **Boss sequence**: order the 3 pivotal passages as they occur in the book (the locust plague and

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/joel.md` applies unchanged.

**New for this tier — reviewed in `docs/reviews/joel-easy-review.md`, bounding confirmed to hold:**

- **Joel 2:28-29 (items 5-6).** This tier's items test only the text's own stated promise ("all
  flesh") and its own stated list of who is included, exactly matching the medium brief's bounding.
  Item 5's multiple-choice distractors ("only the priests," "only the prophets," "only the king")
  test attentive reading of the verse's own scope word ("all flesh") against plausible-sounding but
  textually wrong narrower categories — none of the distractors requires or implies a timing,
  fulfillment, or NT-application claim. No item in this brief asks when this happens or references
  Pentecost, Acts 2, or any NT material, carrying forward the medium brief's explicit instruction.
- **The "day of Yahweh" (item 3, Joel 1:15).** Quotes only the text's own declaration that "the day
  of Yahweh is at hand" as the locust plague's felt significance — no item asks when the day of
  Yahweh occurs or what it consists of beyond what 1:15 itself states.
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — 1:3, 1:4, 1:14, and 2:13 are plain narrative description
  or already-cleared iconic lines with no live cross-tradition disagreement found.
- **Joel 2:30-32 and Joel 3 remain excluded entirely**, per the medium brief's scope decision — no
  verse from either appears anywhere in this brief.

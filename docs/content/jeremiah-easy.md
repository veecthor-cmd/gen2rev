## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple," following the Genesis pilot's design intent
(`docs/content/genesis-easy.md`). This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
each scene and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. All items in this brief sit
at **`difficulty_rank` 1**, with two exceptions carried forward as structural rules from the medium
brief (Jeremiah 1:5 and Jeremiah 31:31-34 stay recall/fill-in-blank only, never multiple-choice —
see Contested Territory). It does **not** simplify or paraphrase verse text — verbatim WEB text
only, identical rule to every other tier.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/jeremiah.md`, no new scenes added

Same 7 scenes: (1) The Call of Jeremiah (Jer 1:4-10, 1:17-19); (2) The Temple Sermon (Jer 7:1-15);
(3) The Potter's House (Jer 18:1-6); (4) Jeremiah and Hananiah — True and False Prophecy (Jer 28);
(5) Jeremiah Imprisoned and Rescued (Jer 37-38); (6) The Fall of Jerusalem, as Jeremiah Witnessed
It (Jer 39); (7) The New Covenant Promise (Jer 31:31-34).

## Challenge Items (12: 9 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (9)

1. **Jer 1:5** (WEB, already-approved verse — **recall/fill-in-blank only, per the medium brief's
   structural rule below**): "Before I formed you in the womb, I knew you. Before you were born, I
   sanctified you. I have appointed you a prophet to the ___." → **nations**.
2. **Jer 1:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle; part of the already-quoted
   "1:6-7" block): "Then I said, 'Ah, Lord Yahweh! Behold, I don't know how to speak; for I am a
   child.'" — MC: "What excuse did Jeremiah give when Yahweh first called him?" Options: "I am a
   child" / "I am unworthy" / "I am blind" / "I am sick." Correct: "I am a child."
3. **Jer 7:3-4** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "'Amend your ways and your
   doings, and I will cause you to dwell in this place. Don't trust in lying words, saying,
   "Yahweh's temple, Yahweh's temple, Yahweh's temple, are these."'" — MC: "What repeated phrase
   did Jeremiah say the people were wrongly trusting in?" Options: "Yahweh's temple, Yahweh's
   temple, Yahweh's temple" / "Peace, peace" / "The day of Yahweh, the day of Yahweh" / "Holy,
   holy, holy." Correct: "Yahweh's temple, Yahweh's temple, Yahweh's temple."
4. **Jer 18:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "'House of Israel, can't I do
   with you as this potter?' says Yahweh. 'Behold, as the clay in the potter's hand, so are you in
   my hand, house of Israel.'" — MC: "What did Yahweh compare the house of Israel to, at the
   potter's house?" Options: clay in a potter's hand / sheep without a shepherd / a vine without a
   vinedresser / gold refined by fire. Correct: clay in a potter's hand.
5. **Jer 28:10-11** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle; part of the already-quoted
   block): "Then Hananiah the prophet took the bar from off the prophet Jeremiah's neck, and broke
   it." — MC: "What did the prophet Hananiah do to the yoke-bar on Jeremiah's neck?" Options: broke
   it off / put it on himself / burned it / buried it. Correct: broke it off.
6. **Jer 38:10** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle; part of the already-quoted
   block): "Then the king commanded Ebedmelech the Ethiopian, saying, 'Take from here thirty men
   with you, and take up Jeremiah the prophet out of the dungeon, before he dies.'" — MC: "Who did
   the king command to rescue Jeremiah from the dungeon?" Options: Ebedmelech the Ethiopian /
   Baruch the scribe / Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard / Zedekiah the king himself. Correct:
   Ebedmelech the Ethiopian. *(Grades who was commanded to rescue Jeremiah and that he did so —
   never the mud or the threat of death itself, per the format restriction below.)*
7. **Jer 39:1-2** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "In the ninth year of
   Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon and all his army came
   against Jerusalem, and besieged it. In the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, the
   ninth day of the month, a breach was made in the city." — MC: "In whose reign as king of Judah
   was Jerusalem's wall finally breached by Babylon?" Options: Zedekiah / Josiah / Jehoiakim /
   Hezekiah. Correct: Zedekiah.
8. **Jer 39:11-12** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Now Nebuchadnezzar king of
   Babylon commanded Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard concerning Jeremiah, saying, 'Take him
   and take care of him. Do him no harm; but do to him even as he tells you.'" — MC: "What did
   Nebuchadnezzar personally command concerning Jeremiah?" Options: Take care of him and do him no
   harm / Send him into exile with the other captives / Put him in prison / Have him killed.
   Correct: Take care of him and do him no harm.
9. **Jer 31:33** (WEB, already-approved verse — **recall/fill-in-blank only, per the medium
   brief's structural rule below**): "'But this is the covenant that I will make with the house of
   Israel after those days,' says Yahweh: 'I will put my law in their inward parts, and I will
   write it in their ___. I will be their God, and they shall be my people.'" → **heart**.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/jeremiah.md` applies unchanged, and every structural
rule and exclusion below is carried forward verbatim from that brief — **no new contested ground
was introduced by this tier's new question angles**, since every item above draws on verse text
already reviewed at `medium`.

- **Jeremiah 1:5 (item 1).** Remains **recall/fill-in-blank only, never multiple-choice**, per the
  medium brief's structural rule addressing this verse's frequent present-day use in
  personhood/abortion-debate discourse. This tier's general MC-preference does not override it —
  item 1 stays recall.
- **Jeremiah 7, the Temple Sermon (items 3, 12).** No New Testament cross-reference to the "den of
  robbers"/temple-cleansing quotation (Matthew 21:13, Mark 11:17, Luke 19:46) appears anywhere in
  this brief, matching the medium brief's scope instruction. Item 12 (Shiloh) is plain historical
  precedent-pointing with no NT connection.
- **Jeremiah 28, testing true and false prophecy (item 5).** Stays on the narrative fact of what
  Hananiah did (broke the yoke-bar) — no item in this tier extends into whether prophecy continues
  today or how to identify a false prophet in the present, matching the medium brief's bounding.
- **Jeremiah 37-38, the cistern imprisonment (item 6).** Grades only who was commanded to rescue
  Jeremiah and that he did so (38:10) — no challenge-ready verse in this tier describes the mud or
  the threat of death directly, matching the medium brief's bounding that graded content lands on
  the rescue, not the peril.
- **Jeremiah 39:6-7 (Zedekiah's sons killed, his eyes put out).** Not selected as challenge-ready
  content anywhere in this brief, confirmed by re-reading every item above — items 7-8 draw only on
  39:1-2 and 39:11-12. **Exclusion holds, unchanged.**
- **Jeremiah 31:31-34, the New Covenant (item 9).** Remains **recall/fill-in-blank only, never
  multiple-choice**, reciting only the verse's own words (including its own stated addressees, "the
  house of Israel... the house of Judah," where quoted) with no claim about later fulfillment or
  present-day applicability. This tier's general MC-preference does not override it — item 9 stays
  recall, and the blank falls on "heart," not on the addressee clause.
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — items 2, 4, 6-8, and 12 are plain narrative description
  or direct quotation with no live cross-tradition disagreement bearing on what's graded.

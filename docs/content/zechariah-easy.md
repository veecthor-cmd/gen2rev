## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
Zechariah's 7 chapters-1-8 passages and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. It
does **not** simplify or paraphrase verse text — the style guide's "verbatim WEB text only" rule
applies identically at every tier. All items are `difficulty_rank` 1.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/zechariah.md`, no new passages added

Same 7 passages, all from chapters 1-8: Zechariah 1:1-6 (Return to Me), 3:1-5 (Vision of Joshua the
High Priest), 4:1-7 (The Golden Lampstand), 5:1-4 (The Flying Scroll), 7:8-10 (True Justice and
Compassion), 8:3-8 (Streets Full of Children), 8:16-17 (Speak the Truth). **Chapters 9-14 remain
entirely out of scope at this tier, same as `medium`** — see Contested Territory below.

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Zechariah 1:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Therefore tell them, Yahweh of
   Armies says: 'Return to me,' says Yahweh of Armies, 'and I will return to you,' says Yahweh of
   Armies." — MC: "What did Yahweh say through Zechariah, calling the people back to him?" Options:
   "Return to me, and I will return to you" / "Come and see" / "Depart from me" / "Stay where you
   are." Correct: "Return to me, and I will return to you." *(The book's opening line, already used
   as a `medium`-tier recall item on the word "Return" — reused here in MC format, testing
   recognition of the whole line rather than one blanked word, matching the Genesis-easy precedent
   of reformatting an already-approved verse for this tier.)*
2. **Zechariah 3:3** (WEB, new): "Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and was standing
   before the angel." — MC: "What was Joshua the high priest wearing when he stood before Yahweh's
   angel?" Options: filthy garments / rich clothing / a linen ephod / armor. Correct: filthy
   garments.
3. **Zechariah 3:5** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "I said, 'Let them set a clean
   turban on his head.' So they set a clean turban on his head, and clothed him; and Yahweh's angel
   was standing by." — MC: "What did they set on Joshua's head after his filthy garments were
   removed?" Options: a clean turban / a crown of gold / a wreath / nothing. Correct: a clean
   turban.
4. **Zechariah 4:2** (WEB, new; trimmed for challenge use — full verse: "He said to me, 'What do
   you see?' I said, 'I have seen, and behold, a lamp stand all of gold, with its bowl on the top of
   it, and its seven lamps on it; there are seven pipes to each of the lamps which are on the top of
   it;'"): "...behold, a lamp stand all of gold..." — MC: "In the vision, what was the lamp stand
   made of?" Options: gold / bronze / silver / clay. Correct: gold.
5. **Zechariah 4:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "Then he answered and spoke to me,
   saying, 'This is Yahweh's word to Zerubbabel, saying, Not by might, nor by power, but by my
   Spirit, says Yahweh of Armies.'" — MC: "According to Zechariah 4:6, by what would Zerubbabel
   accomplish Yahweh's word?" Options: by my Spirit / by might / by wealth / by an army. Correct: by
   my Spirit. *(The book's single most-quoted line, already used as a `medium`-tier recall boss item
   — reused here in MC format, per the same Genesis-easy precedent as item 1.)*
6. **Zechariah 5:2** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "He said to me, 'What do you see?' I
   answered, 'I see a flying scroll; its length is twenty cubits, and its width ten cubits.'" — MC:
   "What did Zechariah see flying in this vision?" Options: a scroll / a lampstand / a chariot / a
   basket. Correct: a scroll.
7. **Zechariah 8:5** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "'The streets of the city will be
   full of boys and girls playing in its streets.'" — MC: "What did Yahweh promise the streets of
   restored Jerusalem would be full of?" Options: boys and girls playing / soldiers marching /
   merchants trading / empty silence. Correct: boys and girls playing.

### Boss (3)

8. **Zechariah 7:10** (WEB, new): "Don't oppress the widow, the fatherless, the foreigner, nor the
   poor; and let none of you devise evil against his brother in your heart.'" — recall: "Don't
   oppress the widow, the fatherless, the ___, nor the poor" → **foreigner**.
9. **Boss sequence — reused verbatim from `medium`**: Order Zechariah 3:1-5 as it happens — "Joshua
   the high priest stands before Yahweh's angel, with Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him
   (3:1)" → "Yahweh rebukes Satan: 'Yahweh rebuke you, Satan!' (3:2)" → "The filthy garments are
   removed from Joshua (3:4)" → "Joshua is clothed with rich clothing and a clean turban is set on
   his head (3:5)".
10. **Boss MC — new**: Zechariah 8:16-17 (WEB): "These are the things that you shall do: speak every
    man the truth with his neighbor. Execute the judgment of truth and peace in your gates, and let
    none of you devise evil in your hearts against his neighbor, and love no false oath; for all
    these are things that I hate," says Yahweh." — "According to Zechariah 8:16-17, what does Yahweh
    say he hates?" Options: devising evil against a neighbor, and loving a false oath / false
    sacrifices / foreign idols / broken promises. Correct: devising evil against a neighbor, and
    loving a false oath.

*(Items 1 and 5 reuse already-reviewed verses from `medium` in a new MC format, per the pilot's own
Genesis-easy precedent — deliberately, to keep the two most quoted lines of chs. 1-8 ("Return to me")
and 4:6's "Not by might, nor by power, but by my Spirit") present at every tier. Item 9 is an exact
reuse of an already-reviewed `medium` boss item, to keep the vision's most dramatic beat consistent
across tiers. Every other item (2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 8, 10) is a genuinely new item drawn from a verse the
`medium` tier's 17 items never used standalone.)*

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/zechariah.md` applies unchanged and is **re-confirmed to
hold for this tier**:

- **No verse from Zechariah 9:9, 11:12-13, 12:10, or 13:7 appears anywhere in this brief.** No item
  above draws on any material from chapters 9-14.
- **No verse from Zechariah 3:8-10 or 6:12-13 ("the Branch") appears anywhere in this brief.** Item 3
  stops, like `medium`, at 3:5 — the vision's "Branch" material (3:8-10) is never reached.
- **Zechariah 3:1's "Satan" reference** (embedded only in item 9's reused sequence text, exactly as
  already reviewed in `medium`) stays narrative-only — an adversary who accuses Joshua and is
  rebuked, no characterization beyond the text's own narration. No new item in this brief introduces
  the word "Satan" independently.
- **No new contested ground identified.** Every newly-selected verse in this brief (1:3 reused, 3:3,
  3:5 reused, 4:2, 4:6 reused, 5:2 reused, 7:10, 8:5 reused, 8:16-17) is plain narrative description
  or direct ethical exhortation, already checked and confirmed safe in `docs/reviews/zechariah-review.md`
  §3.5-3.7 (the "Branch" bounding, the chs. 9-14 scope boundary, and the "Satan" bounding) — no live
  cross-tradition disagreement attaches to any of them independent of what that review already found.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge," following the Genesis pilot's design
intent (`docs/content/genesis-hard.md`). This tier draws on verses that are narratively significant
but less likely to be immediately recognized — precise numbers, secondary character names, and
exact phrasing — and requires typed recall (no multiple-choice scaffolding) for every regular item.
All items in this brief sit at **`difficulty_rank` 5**. Same guardrails as every other tier:
verbatim WEB text only, narrative fact only, no doctrinal position required.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/esther.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes as `medium` and `easy`: (1) Esther Becomes Queen (Esther 2), (2) Haman's Plot — The
Decree Against the Jews (Esther 3), (3) "For Such a Time as This" — Esther's Decision (Esther 4),
(4) Esther's Banquets and Haman's Downfall (Esther 5-7), (5) Reversal and the Establishment of
Purim (Esther 8-9).

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/esther.md` applies unchanged: the absence of God's name
from the text; the "gathering of virgins" / royal harem context; the genocide plot and its violent
reversal. **No new contested ground for this tier** — every new verse (2:21-22, 3:7, 5:14, 6:1, 8:8)
is plain narrative/procedural detail with no live cross-tradition disagreement found.

**Structural rules carried forward, confirmed to hold for this tier:**
- **Esther 2:7, 2:15, and 2:17 remain restricted to recall/fill-in-blank format only, never
  multiple-choice.** This tier's use of a restricted verse (item 1, Esther 2:7) is already recall by
  design, since this entire tier is recall-heavy — the restriction is honored without needing to
  override the tier's own default format.
- **Esther 9:6-10 (Haman's ten sons named individually) and Esther 9:16 (the 75,000 casualty
  figure) remain excluded from all challenge-ready content at this tier, as at every tier.** The
  finer-grained boss sequence (item 9) still states the reversal only at the level the medium brief
  established ("the Jews prevail") — it does not narrate or quote either excluded passage.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge." This tier draws on verses that are
theologically or narratively significant but less likely to be immediately recognized — exact
covenant wording, secondary details, and precise phrasing — and requires typed recall (no
multiple-choice scaffolding) wherever the format allows. It stays inside the same guardrails as
every other tier: verbatim WEB text only, narrative fact only, no doctrinal position required.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/genesis.md` applies unchanged.

**New for this tier — reviewed against live cross-tradition sources in
`docs/reviews/genesis-hard-review.md` §3, bounding confirmed to hold in every case:**

- **Genesis 1:26, "Let's make man in our image, after our likeness."** The plural pronoun ("us,"
  "our") is a genuine, confirmed point of cross-tradition discussion distinct from 1:27's "male and
  female" question: Jewish commentary (Rashi, via Sefaria) reads it as God addressing the angelic
  court; the Catholic NABRE's own footnote (USCCB) describes a heavenly-assembly reading; classic
  Protestant commentators are internally divided (Henry/Barnes/Gill read a Trinitarian hint; Poole
  and Keil & Delitzsch reject it); contemporary evangelical sources favor the Trinitarian reading.
  **Structural rule: this item is recall-only (fill-in-blank on "image"), never multiple-choice;
  "us"/"our" may never themselves be the blanked token; and no challenge may ask what "us/our"
  refers to.** Confirmed sufficient because "in our image, after our likeness" renders identically
  across every disagreeing tradition (Revised JPS matches WEB here) — the graded token itself
  carries no tradition-specific loading (Blue Letter Bible, H6754).
- **Genesis 1:2, "God's Spirit was hovering over the surface of the waters."** Not flagged in the
  original draft; added per theological review. WEB's "God's Spirit" is itself a translation choice
  — Revised JPS renders the same Hebrew (*ruach elohim*) as "a wind from God," and the Catholic
  NABRE's main text reads "a mighty wind" with a footnote noting the literal "spirit or breath of
  God." **Structural rule: the blank stays on the "formless and empty" clause only; the "God's
  Spirit"/wind clause is display-only and is never the graded token or a multiple-choice subject.**
- **Genesis 17:5** sits inside Genesis 17, the covenant-of-circumcision chapter. Confirmed via the
  text itself that circumcision isn't introduced until 17:10, five verses later with no shared
  clause — v5 contains only the renaming and its stated reason. Cross-tradition sources (Sefaria/
  Rashi, Bible Hub's aggregated classic commentators) treat v5 itself as an onomastic/etymological
  matter, not a doctrinal one. **Structural rule: only the name-change clause is graded ("Abraham");
  "father of a multitude of nations" — the clause Paul quotes in Romans 4:17, live Catholic/
  Protestant ground — is never a challenge subject in any tier, and circumcision itself is never
  challenge-ready content anywhere in this game.**
- **Genesis 22:2** stays strictly on *what God said to Abraham* (the instruction itself, quoted) —
  never *why* God asked it. Same bounding as the rest of the Gen 22 material.

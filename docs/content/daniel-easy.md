## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple," following the Genesis pilot's design intent
(`docs/content/genesis-easy.md`). This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
each of Daniel's 6 pivotal scenes and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. All
items in this brief sit at **`difficulty_rank` 1**. It does **not** simplify or paraphrase verse
text — verbatim WEB text only, identical rule to every other tier. No item in this brief touches
the dream's kingdom-identification (Daniel 2) or any content from chapters 7-12.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/daniel.md`, no new scenes added, all within ch. 1-6

Same 6 scenes: (1) Daniel and His Friends Refuse the King's Food (Dan 1); (2) Nebuchadnezzar's
Dream and Daniel's Interpretation (Dan 2); (3) The Fiery Furnace (Dan 3); (4) Nebuchadnezzar's
Pride and Restoration (Dan 4); (5) Belshazzar's Feast and the Writing on the Wall (Dan 5);
(6) Daniel in the Lions' Den (Dan 6).

**Daniel 7-12 remain entirely excluded — no verse, scene description, or reference to their content
anywhere in this brief.**

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Dan 1:8** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "But Daniel purposed in his heart
   that he would not defile himself with the king's delicacies, nor with the wine which he drank.
   Therefore he requested of the prince of the eunuchs that he might not defile himself." — MC:
   "What did Daniel resolve not to do?" Options: defile himself with the king's food and wine /
   disobey the king in every matter / refuse to learn Babylonian wisdom / refuse his new name.
   Correct: defile himself with the king's food and wine.
2. **Dan 1:12** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "'Test your servants, I beg you,
   ten days; and let them give us vegetables to eat and water to drink.'" — MC: "For how many days
   did Daniel ask the guard to test him and his friends?" Options: ten days / seven days / thirty
   days / three years. Correct: ten days.
3. **Dan 2:47** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "The king answered to Daniel,
   and said, 'Of a truth your God is the God of gods, and the Lord of kings, and a revealer of
   secrets, since you have been able to reveal this secret.'" — MC: "What did King Nebuchadnezzar
   say about Daniel's God after the dream was revealed?" Options: "your God is the God of gods, and
   the Lord of kings" / "your God is weaker than mine" / he said nothing about God / "your God is
   only a story." Correct: "your God is the God of gods, and the Lord of kings." *(Narrative fact
   about the king's own statement only — no item requires identifying what any part of the dream
   represents; see Contested Territory below.)*
4. **Dan 3:16-18** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Shadrach, Meshach, and
   Abednego answered the king, 'Nebuchadnezzar, we have no need to answer you in this matter... But
   if not, let it be known to you, O king, that we will not serve your gods or worship the golden
   image which you have set up.'" — MC: "What did Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego tell the king
   they would not do?" Options: worship the golden image / leave Babylon / eat the king's food /
   bow to Daniel. Correct: worship the golden image.
5. **Dan 3:25** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "'Look, I see four men loose,
   walking in the middle of the fire, and they are unharmed. The appearance of the fourth is like a
   son of the gods.'" — MC: "What did Nebuchadnezzar see when he looked into the furnace?" Options:
   four men, unharmed, walking in the fire / three men, badly burned / an angel standing alone /
   smoke and nothing else. Correct: four men, unharmed, walking in the fire.
6. **Dan 5:25** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "This is the writing that was inscribed:
   'MENE, MENE, TEKEL, UPHARSIN.'" — recall: "This is the writing that was inscribed: 'MENE, MENE,
   TEKEL, ___.'" → **UPHARSIN**.
7. **Dan 6:16** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Then the king commanded, and
   they brought Daniel and cast him into the den of lions. The king spoke and said to Daniel, 'Your
   God whom you serve continually, he will deliver you.'" — MC: "Where was Daniel thrown for
   continuing to pray?" Options: the den of lions / a furnace / a prison cell / a pit of snakes.
   Correct: the den of lions.

### Boss (3)

8. **Dan 6:22** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "'My God has sent his angel, and
   has shut the lions' mouths, and they have not hurt me, because innocence was found in me before
   him; and also before you, O king, I have done no harm.'" — MC: "According to Daniel, who kept
   the lions from hurting him?" Options: God's angel, who shut the lions' mouths / the king's guards
   / Daniel's own strength / no one — the lions were already tame. Correct: God's angel, who shut
   the lions' mouths.
9. **Dan 1:15** (WEB, already-approved verse, new format): "At the end of ten days, their faces
   appeared fairer and they were fatter in flesh than all the youths who ate of the king's
   delicacies." — recall: "At the end of ten days, their faces appeared ___ and they were fatter in
   flesh than all the youths who ate of the king's delicacies." → **fairer**.
10. **Boss sequence — reused from `medium`'s own Level 3 example**: Order the pivotal scenes as
    they appear in the book: food test → dream interpretation → fiery furnace → pride and
    restoration → writing on the wall → lions' den.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
each of Ezra's 5 scenes and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall, so a first-time
or younger player can succeed without prior Bible knowledge. It does **not** simplify or paraphrase
verse text — the style guide's "verbatim WEB text only" rule applies identically at every tier.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/ezra.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes as `medium`: (1) Cyrus's Decree and the Return (Ezra 1); (2) Rebuilding the Altar and
the Temple Foundation (Ezra 3); (3) Opposition, the Work Resumes, and the Temple Completed
(Ezra 4-6); (4) Ezra the Scribe: Devotion to the Law (Ezra 7); (5) National Repentance Over
Intermarriage (Ezra 9-10). **Scene 5's exclusion-based bounding — resolved by Kachi Okere on
2026-08-25, see the addendum in `docs/reviews/ezra-review.md` — carries forward unchanged: Ezra
9:1-2, 10:3, 10:10-12, and 10:44 remain permanently excluded from challenge-ready content at every
tier, not just `medium`.** No item below draws on any of those four excluded verses.

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Ezra 1:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Whoever there is among you of
   all his people, may his God be with him, and let him go up to Jerusalem, which is in Judah, and
   build the house of Yahweh, the God of Israel (he is God), which is in Jerusalem." — MC:
   "According to Ezra 1:3, what does the verse say about Yahweh, the God of Israel, in the
   parentheses?" Options: "he is God" / "he is merciful" / "he is one" / "he is holy". Correct:
   "he is God." *(Different clause than medium's own difficulty-ladder illustration of this verse —
   that example asks what the proclamation authorized the exiles to do; this asks about the
   parenthetical, a distinct fact from the same verse.)*
2. **Ezra 1:7** (WEB, new): "Also Cyrus the king brought out the vessels of Yahweh's house, which
   Nebuchadnezzar had brought out of Jerusalem, and had put in the house of his gods;" — MC:
   "According to Ezra 1:7, who had originally taken the temple vessels out of Jerusalem?" Options:
   Nebuchadnezzar / Cyrus / Darius / Sheshbazzar. Correct: Nebuchadnezzar.
3. **Ezra 3:4** (WEB, new): "They kept the feast of booths, as it is written, and offered the daily
   burnt offerings by number, according to the ordinance, as the duty of every day required;" — MC:
   "According to Ezra 3:4, which feast did the returned exiles keep after rebuilding the altar?"
   Options: the feast of booths / Passover / the feast of weeks / the Day of Atonement. Correct: the
   feast of booths.
4. **Ezra 3:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "In spite of their fear because
   of the peoples of the surrounding lands, they set the altar on its base; and they offered burnt
   offerings on it to Yahweh, even burnt offerings morning and evening." — MC: "According to Ezra
   3:3, what were the returned exiles afraid of?" Options: the peoples of the surrounding lands /
   Persian soldiers / wild animals / a famine. Correct: the peoples of the surrounding lands.
5. **Ezra 6:16** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "The children of Israel, the
   priests, the Levites, and the rest of the children of the captivity, kept the dedication of this
   house of God with joy." — MC: "According to Ezra 6:16, how did the people keep the dedication of
   the finished temple?" Options: with joy / with mourning / in complete silence / only with
   fasting. Correct: with joy.
6. **Ezra 7:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "...this Ezra went up from
   Babylon. He was a skilled scribe in the law of Moses, which Yahweh, the God of Israel, had given;
   and the king granted him all his request, according to Yahweh his God's hand on him." — MC:
   "According to Ezra 7:6, what was Ezra known as?" Options: a skilled scribe in the law of Moses /
   a mighty warrior / the king's cupbearer / a builder of walls. Correct: a skilled scribe in the
   law of Moses.
7. **Ezra 9:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "When I heard this thing, I tore
   my garment and my robe, and pulled the hair out of my head and of my beard, and sat down
   confounded." — MC: "According to Ezra 9:3, what did Ezra do when he heard of the people's
   unfaithfulness?" Options: he tore his garment and pulled out his hair / he celebrated with a
   feast / he left Jerusalem immediately / he wrote a letter to the king. Correct: he tore his
   garment and pulled out his hair. *(Confession/reaction content only, within Scene 5's approved
   bounding — never asks the player to process the divorce/expulsion outcome; see Contested
   Territory below.)*

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/ezra.md` applies unchanged, including the Scene 5
mass-divorce bounding resolved by Kachi Okere (see that brief's status header and
`docs/reviews/ezra-review.md`'s addendum).

**Confirmed for this tier:**

- **Scene 5 (item 7)** stays strictly within the already-approved confession/repentance verses
  (9:3) and never touches the excluded divorce/expulsion clauses (9:1-2, 10:3, 10:10-12, 10:44) or
  asks the player to process the fate of the wives or children. No new bounding decision was
  required — this item sits comfortably inside the existing, human-resolved scope.
- **Ezra 1:1's prophecy-fulfillment structural rule (recall-only format)** is not engaged this tier
  — 1:1 is not used in any item above. The restriction remains binding if a future revision adds it.
- **Chronology of Ezra and Nehemiah.** Item 9 (boss sequence) sequences events only within this
  book's own internal narrative order (Scenes 1-4), never against a reconstructed cross-book
  timeline — same bounding as `medium`.
- **Opposition from "the peoples of the land" (Ezra 4).** Still not selected as challenge-ready
  content — no item above names or characterizes this group beyond the general fact of opposition
  (already covered narratively in Scene 3's description, not in a graded item).
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — every other new-format or newly-selected verse (1:7, 3:3,
  3:4, 6:16, 7:6, 7:9) is plain narrative description with no live cross-tradition disagreement
  found.

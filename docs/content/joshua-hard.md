## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi's "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge" direction. This tier draws on less
commonly memorized, secondary details from the SAME 5 scenes — exact wording, place names, and
precise phrasing — and requires typed recall (no multiple-choice scaffolding) wherever the format
allows. As with the easy tier, and for the same reason (Joshua's especially sensitive Contested
Territory), this tier draws **only** on verse text already quoted verbatim and reviewed in the
medium brief. Every "less commonly memorized" fact below (the city of Adam beside Zarethan, the Red
Sea detail in Rahab's speech, the phrase "on dry land," Caleb's "as strong today") is a genuine
secondary detail already sitting inside an already-approved multi-clause quotation — this tier simply
blanks a different word than the easy tier or medium brief did.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/joshua.md`, no new scenes added

Same 5 scenes as `medium` and `easy`: Crossing the Jordan (Joshua 3-4), Rahab and the Spies (Joshua
2), The Fall of Jericho (Joshua 6), The Conquest Campaigns (Joshua 10-11, geographic/summary verses
only), Dividing the Land (Joshua 14, 21:43-45).

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/joshua.md` applies unchanged. **Nothing new is
introduced by this brief**: no new verse, no new scene. Self-check below, same method as the easy
tier's brief.

- **Ḥerem/destruction-clause verses (6:17, 6:21, 10:40) — confirmed absent.** None of the 10 items
  above quote, paraphrase, or reference this text, including item 6 (6:4, display context 6:3-5) and
  item 7 (6:25), which stop at the same non-excluded verses the medium brief approved.
- **Scene 4 (Conquest Campaigns) format compliance.** Item 10 (this tier's only Scene 4 item) is
  recall/fill-in-blank, grades only the place name "Goshen" (per the R1 fix applied above), and makes
  no claim about motive or justification. **Compliant** with the existing structural rule.
- **Scene 5, Josh 21:43-45 format compliance.** Item 9 (this tier's only 21:43-45 item, reused
  verbatim from `easy`) is recall/fill-in-blank and makes no present-day claim. **Compliant.** The
  21:43-45 covenant-vs-dispensationalism fulfilment dispute (documented in
  `docs/content/joshua-easy.md`'s Contested Territory section, added there per its review Finding D)
  binds this brief too: the quantifier "all" in 21:43 may never be the blanked token, and no item at
  any tier may ask whether the promise was completely fulfilled.
- **Joshua 2:10 ḥerem-vocabulary note — added per review R3, required.** Item 2's display text is
  locked to the trimmed form shown above (ending "...when you came out of Egypt..."). The clause "and
  what you did to the two kings of the Amorites, who were beyond the Jordan, to Sihon and to Og, whom
  you utterly destroyed" — the second half of 2:10, confirmed to contain ḥerem vocabulary (Revised JPS
  renders the same clause "whom you doomed," the standard rendering of the ḥerem verb) — is never
  display text, never a graded token, and never a challenge subject at any tier. This note is also
  carried to `docs/content/joshua-easy.md` for completeness (that brief does not use 2:9-11 and is
  unaffected in practice, but the exclusion list should be complete rather than tier-local).
- **Joshua 3:16, "at Adam" — forward guard added per review Rec-2.** The Masoretic text carries a
  ketiv/qere at this point (written "at/in Adam," read "from Adam"), so translations legitimately
  differ on the preposition. No effect on item 1, which grades the stable place name. **No challenge at
  any tier may grade "at" versus "from" Adam.**
- **Joshua 4:22, "on dry ___" — near-miss recorded per review R2.** 3:17 renders the same idea "on dry
  ground," and this brief's own item 8 sequence card uses "on dry ground." Item 3 is now anchored
  ("According to Joshua 4:22...") and the grading/ingestion pipeline must record "ground" as an
  explicitly-rejected near-miss rather than silently accepting or silently rejecting it.
- **No other new contested ground.** All seven regular items reuse clauses from verse blocks already
  verified character-for-character in `docs/reviews/joshua-review.md` §1 — this brief only changes
  which word inside each already-approved quotation is blanked.

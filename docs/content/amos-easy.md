## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple." This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts from
Amos's 4 pivotal passages and favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. It does **not**
simplify or paraphrase verse text. Difficulty skews to rank 1 throughout.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/amos.md`, no new scenes added

Same 4: The Judgment Oracles Against the Nations — and Israel Itself (1-2); Economic Injustice Named
Directly (5:11-12); "Let Justice Roll on Like Rivers" (5:21-24); Amos's Own Call — "I Was No Prophet"
(7:14-15).

## Challenge Items (9: 8 regular + 1 boss)

### Regular (8, all multiple-choice, difficulty_rank 1)

1. **Amos 1:3** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Yahweh says: 'For three
   transgressions of Damascus, yes, for four, I will not turn away its punishment, because they have
   threshed Gilead with threshing instruments of iron.'" — MC: "According to Amos 1:3, which nation is the first
   addressed in the judgment-oracle cycle?" Options: Damascus / Edom / Moab / Israel. Correct:
   Damascus. *(Identification-level only — which nation is addressed — per the in-bounds category the
   medium brief itself names for the seven non-Israel nations.)*
2. **Amos 2:6** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "Yahweh says: 'For three
   transgressions of Israel, yes, for four, I will not turn away its punishment, because they have sold
   the righteous for silver, and the needy for a pair of sandals.'" — MC: "According to Amos 2:6, what did Israel do
   to 'the needy,' per the text?" Options: sold them for a pair of sandals / gave them bread / freed
   them / taxed them. Correct: sold them for a pair of sandals.
3. **Amos 2:7, first sentence only** (WEB, already-approved verse; the second sentence is never
   quoted, per the exclusion below): "They trample the heads of the poor into the dust of the earth
   and deny justice to the oppressed." — MC: "According to Amos 2:7, what does the text say they do
   to 'the heads of the poor'?" Options: trample them into the dust / lift them up / ignore them /
   feed them. Correct: trample them into the dust.
4. **Amos 5:12** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "For I know how many are your
   offenses, and how great are your sins—you who afflict the just, who take a bribe, and who turn
   away the needy in the courts." — MC: "According to Amos 5:12, what does the text say some
   people 'take' unjustly?" Options: a bribe / a wife / a house / a name. Correct: a bribe.
5. **Amos 5:21** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "I hate, I despise your feasts,
   and I can't stand your solemn assemblies." — MC: "According to Amos 5:21, what does Yahweh say
   about the feasts?" Options: "I hate, I despise your feasts" / "I love your feasts" / "I ignore
   your feasts" / "I bless your feasts." Correct: "I hate, I despise your feasts."
6. **Amos 5:24** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle, the book's most iconic line;
   note the verified WEB wording is "roll on," not the more famous NRSV/RSV "roll down"): "But let
   justice roll on like rivers, and righteousness like a mighty stream." — MC: "According to Amos
   5:24 (WEB), what does Amos say should 'roll on like rivers'?" Options: justice / mercy / wisdom /
   peace. Correct: justice.
7. **Amos 7:14** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle, iconic): "I was no prophet,
   neither was I a prophet's son, but I was a herdsman, and a farmer of sycamore figs." — MC:
   "According to Amos 7:14, what was Amos's occupation before he was called to prophesy?" Options: a
   herdsman and a farmer of sycamore figs / a priest / a king's scribe / a soldier. Correct: a
   herdsman and a farmer of sycamore figs.
8. **Amos 7:15** (WEB, already-approved verse, new question angle): "and Yahweh took me from
   following the flock, and Yahweh said to me, 'Go, prophesy to my people Israel.'" — MC: "According
   to Amos 7:15, what did Yahweh tell Amos to do?" Options: "Go, prophesy to my people Israel." /
   "Go, build a temple." / "Go, gather an army." / "Go, return to Tekoa." Correct: "Go, prophesy to
   my people Israel."

### Boss (1)

9. **Boss sequence**: order the 4 pivotal passages as they occur in the book (the judgment oracles
   against the nations and Israel → economic injustice named directly → "let justice roll on like
   rivers" → Amos's own call).

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/amos.md` applies unchanged.

**New for this tier — reviewed in `docs/reviews/amos-easy-review.md`, bounding confirmed to hold:**

- **Amos 9:11-12 remains excluded entirely — no verse from it appears anywhere in this brief**,
  matching the medium brief's decision.
- **Amos 2:7's second sentence ("a man and his father use the same maiden...") is never quoted in
  this brief.** Item 3 above quotes only the first sentence, exactly as at medium.
- **The judgment oracles' severity (item 1).** Used only at the identification level — which nation
  is addressed — never at the level of endorsing or evaluating the announced punishment, same
  bounding as medium.
- **Amos 5:24's wording (item 6).** The medium review flagged this exact risk — that the popular
  NRSV/RSV phrasing ("roll down like waters... an ever-flowing stream") could be mistakenly
  substituted for the actual WEB text ("roll on like rivers... a mighty stream"). This item's stem
  and options use only the verified WEB wording; the brief explicitly flags the wording distinction
  in-line as an extra safeguard against the same mistake recurring at this tier.
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — 1:3, 2:6, 5:12, 5:21, 7:14, and 7:15 are plain narrative
  description or already-cleared iconic lines with no live cross-tradition disagreement found.

## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "easy... real simple," following the Genesis pilot's design intent
(`docs/content/genesis-easy.md`). This tier draws the most iconic, broadly-recognizable facts and
favors multiple-choice recognition over typed recall. All items in this brief sit at
**`difficulty_rank` 1**. It does **not** simplify or paraphrase verse text — verbatim WEB text
only, identical rule to every other tier. Where the medium brief restricts a specific verse to
recall-only format (Job 1:21, 19:25-27, 42:6), that restriction is honored here even though it
means those individual items don't follow this tier's general multiple-choice preference.

## Scenes/Passages — reused from `docs/content/job.md`, no new scenes or passages added

**Narrative Frame Scenes (3):** (1) Job's Prosperity and the Test Begins (Job 1:1-3, 1:6-12);
(2) Job's Losses and His Response (Job 1:13-22, 2:7-10); (3) Job's Response and Restoration
(Job 42:1-8, 42:10-17).

**Pivotal Passages from the Poetic Middle (4):** (1) God's First Speech: The Foundations of the
Earth (Job 38:1-7); (2) God's Speech: The Sea, Death, and the Cosmos (Job 38:16-18, 38:31-33);
(3) God's Speech: The Warhorse (Job 39:19-25); (4) Job's Hope: "I Know That My Redeemer Lives"
(Job 19:25-27).

As at `medium`, the ~34-chapter dialogue and Elihu's speech (Job 3-37), Behemoth and Leviathan
(Job 40:15-41:34), and the explicit death-report verses (Job 1:18-19) supply **no challenge-ready
text anywhere in this brief** — same reasoning as the medium brief, restated in Contested Territory
below rather than re-litigated.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/job.md` applies unchanged: the core assignment
guardrail (no item may ask a player to affirm or reject a position on why God allows suffering, or
whether Job's friends' theology was right or wrong); "The Satan" figure (Job 1:6-12); Job 1:21;
Job 19:25-27; Job 42:6; Job 42:7; and all deliberately excluded material. **No new contested ground
for this tier** — every new verse (1:1, 38:31, 1:8, 2:9, 38:4, 39:19, 42:10) is plain narrative
description or God's-speech imagery already covered by the medium brief's analysis.

**New finding for this tier, not doctrinal but worth recording:** the two live WEB sources this
brief drew from disagree at the character level on Job 1:8 — ebible.org renders "there is no one
like him **on** the earth," while biblegateway.com (`version=WEB`) renders "there is no one like
him **in** the earth." Neither reading changes meaning or touches any doctrinal question; this is a
minor edition/revision difference between the two sites' copies of WEB, not a translation dispute.
This brief uses "in the earth," matching the wording `docs/content/job.md` (medium) already cites,
for consistency with the already-approved citation. **Flagged explicitly for the verbatim-text QA
pass (`QA_REQUIREMENTS.md` §3): confirm which wording the project's canonical WEB source of record
actually prints before ingestion**, since this brief did not need to (and could not, from two
disagreeing live sources) adjudicate it independently.

**Structural rules carried forward, confirmed to hold for this tier:**
- **"The Satan" figure (Job 1:6-12): items must be recall/fill-in-blank or narrative-fact multiple
  choice about actions/dialogue only — never a question asking who/what Satan is.** Item 3 (1:8)
  is Yahweh's dialogue about Job, not an identity question about Satan — compliant.
- **Job 1:21, Job 19:25-27, and Job 42:6 remain recall/fill-in-blank format only, never
  multiple-choice.** Items 5 and 8 above (42:6 and 19:25) honor this; this tier's general
  MC-preference does not override it.
- **Job 42:7 (God's rebuke of the friends) is not used as a standalone graded item in this brief**
  — consistent with the medium brief's narrow framing (rebuke happened, and to whom, never why);
  this tier does not add a new item on it.
- **Job 3-37, Job 40:15-41:34 (Behemoth/Leviathan), and Job 1:18-19 remain fully excluded — no
  verse from any of these ranges appears above.**

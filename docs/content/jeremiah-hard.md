## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge," following the Genesis pilot's design
intent (`docs/content/genesis-hard.md`). This tier draws on less commonly memorized, more precise
clauses — exact names, numbers, and phrasing — from within the same already-approved scenes, and
requires typed recall (no multiple-choice scaffolding) for every regular item. All items in this
brief sit at **`difficulty_rank` 5**. Same guardrails as every other tier: verbatim WEB text only,
narrative fact only, no doctrinal position required.

## Pivotal Scenes — reused from `docs/content/jeremiah.md`, no new scenes added

Same 7 scenes as `medium` and `easy`: (1) The Call of Jeremiah (Jer 1:4-10, 1:17-19); (2) The
Temple Sermon (Jer 7:1-15); (3) The Potter's House (Jer 18:1-6); (4) Jeremiah and Hananiah — True
and False Prophecy (Jer 28); (5) Jeremiah Imprisoned and Rescued (Jer 37-38); (6) The Fall of
Jerusalem, as Jeremiah Witnessed It (Jer 39); (7) The New Covenant Promise (Jer 31:31-34).

**Three verses below (1:18, 28:1, 39:14) sit inside these same scenes' already-approved episodes
but were not individually quoted at `medium`** — 1:18 is the verse immediately following the
already-quoted 1:19 within the same Call-of-Jeremiah episode (1:17-19); 28:1 is Hananiah's own
introduction within the already-scoped Jeremiah 28 episode; 39:14 is the immediate continuation of
the already-quoted 39:11-12 command, inside the same Jeremiah 39 episode. No item below extends
into any scene's material beyond what medium already scoped.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/jeremiah.md` applies unchanged, and every structural
rule and exclusion below is carried forward. **No new doctrinal ground was introduced by the three
newly-quoted verses (1:18, 28:1, 39:14)** — all three are plain narrative/descriptive continuations
of already-reviewed episodes, confirmed against live sources during drafting (see
`docs/reviews/jeremiah-hard-review.md` §1 for citations).

- **Jeremiah 1:5 (item 1).** Remains **recall/fill-in-blank only, never multiple-choice** — this
  entire tier is recall-by-default for regular items, so the restriction is naturally satisfied;
  stated explicitly because it's the same structural rule carried from medium, not a new one.
- **Jeremiah 1:18 (item 2).** Narrative continuation of the already-reviewed call narrative (the
  "fortified city... iron pillar... bronze walls" image immediately precedes the already-quoted
  1:19 within the same verse span 1:17-19). No new contested ground — this is Yahweh's promise of
  Jeremiah's own resilience, not a claim requiring cross-tradition adjudication.
- **Jeremiah 7 (items 3-4).** No New Testament cross-reference to the temple-cleansing quotation
  appears anywhere in this brief, matching the medium brief's scope instruction. Item 3 (7:9-11,
  "Baal") and item 4 (7:12, "Shiloh") are both plain narrative/quoted-speech recall.
- **Jeremiah 28 (item 6).** Hananiah's lineage ("son of Azzur") and hometown ("of Gibeon") are
  narrative-identification detail with no cross-tradition doctrinal content — confirmed no source
  disputes this genealogical note. No item in this tier extends into whether prophecy continues
  today, matching the medium brief's bounding.
- **Jeremiah 37-38 (item 7).** Grades only the word "dungeon" naming where Jeremiah was placed —
  no challenge-ready verse in this tier describes the mud or elaborates the threat of death beyond
  the single already-approved clause "likely to die... because of the famine," which itself was
  already part of the medium brief's approved 38:9 citation. Matches the medium brief's bounding
  that graded content lands on the rescue, not the peril.
- **Jeremiah 39:6-7 (Zedekiah's sons killed, his eyes put out).** Not selected as challenge-ready
  content anywhere in this brief, confirmed by re-reading every item above — items 8 and 12 draw
  only on 39:14 and 39:17-18, both outside the 39:6-7 range. **Exclusion holds, unchanged.**
- **Jeremiah 39:14 (item 8), Gedaliah.** Plain narrative-identification detail (a name and a
  patronymic); Gedaliah's later governorship and assassination (Jeremiah 40-41) are not part of
  this book's approved scene list and are not referenced here. No new contested ground.
- **Jeremiah 31:31-34, the New Covenant (item 9).** Remains **recall/fill-in-blank only, never
  multiple-choice**, reciting only the verse's own words, with no claim about later fulfillment or
  present-day applicability. This tier's recall-by-default format makes the restriction moot in
  practice, but it is stated explicitly as still binding.
- **No new contested ground otherwise.**

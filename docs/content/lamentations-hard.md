## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge," following the Genesis pilot's design
intent (`docs/content/genesis-hard.md`). This tier draws on less commonly memorized, more precise
clauses from within the same already-approved passages and requires typed recall (no
multiple-choice scaffolding) for every regular item. All items in this brief sit at
**`difficulty_rank` 5**. Same guardrails as every other tier: verbatim WEB text only, narrative
fact only, no doctrinal position required. Stays as deliberately thin as `easy` (9 items total),
the same proportionality the medium brief itself established for this short, heavily-excluded
book.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/lamentations.md`, no new passages added

Same 3 passages as `medium` and `easy`: (1) The Opening Lament (Lam 1:1-2, 1:12); (2) The Turn to
Hope, at the Book's Darkest Point (Lam 3:18-24); (3) The Book's Closing Plea (Lam 5:19-21).

## Challenge Items (9: 6 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (6)

1. **Lam 1:1** (WEB, already-approved verse, different blank than `easy`): "How the city sits
   solitary, that was full of people! She has become as a widow, who was great among the nations!
   She who was a princess among the provinces has become a ___!" → **slave**.
2. **Lam 1:2** (WEB, already-approved verse, trimmed): "She weeps bitterly in the night. Her tears
   are on her cheeks. Among all her lovers she has no one to ___ her. All her friends have dealt
   treacherously with her. They have become her enemies." → **comfort**.
3. **Lam 1:12** (WEB, already-approved verse, different clause than `easy`): "'Is it nothing to
   you, all you who pass by? Look, and see if there is any sorrow like my sorrow, which is brought
   on me, with which Yahweh has afflicted me in the day of his fierce ___.'" → **anger**.
4. **Lam 3:19-20** (WEB, already-approved verse): "Remember my affliction and my misery, the ___
   and the bitterness. My soul still remembers them, and is bowed down within me." → **wormwood**.
5. **Lam 3:24** (WEB, already-approved verse): "'Yahweh is my portion,' says my soul. 'Therefore I
   will ___ in him.'" → **hope**.
6. **Lam 5:20** (WEB, already-approved verse): "Why do you forget us forever, and forsake us for
   so long a ___?" → **time**.

### Boss (3)

7. **Boss sequence (new, finer-grained than `easy`/`medium`)**: order the beats across all three
   passages — "the city sits solitary" (Lam 1:1) → "she weeps bitterly in the night" (Lam 1:2) →
   "she asks all who pass by to look at her sorrow" (Lam 1:12) → "the speaker's strength and hope
   have perished" (Lam 3:18) → "he recalls Yahweh's mercies and turns to hope" (Lam 3:21-23) →
   "'Yahweh is my portion,' he says" (Lam 3:24) → "Yahweh's throne remains forever" (Lam 5:19) →
   "why have you forgotten us so long?" (Lam 5:20) → "turn us back to yourself, Yahweh" (Lam 5:21).
8. **Boss quotation-matching — reused verbatim from `medium`'s own Level-4 ladder example**:
   given three quotations (Lam 1:12, Lam 3:22-23, Lam 5:21), place them in the order they occur and
   match each to its reference.
9. **Lam 3:21-23** (WEB, already-approved verse, different blank than `easy`'s item 5, which
   blanked "morning"): "This I recall to my mind; therefore I have hope. It is because of Yahweh's
   loving kindnesses that we are not ___, because his mercies don't fail. They are new every
   morning. Great is your faithfulness." → **consumed**.

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/lamentations.md` applies unchanged, and every
structural rule and exclusion below is carried forward verbatim from that brief — **no new
contested ground was introduced by this tier's new blanks**, since every item above draws on verse
text already reviewed at `medium` and no item extends any passage's boundary.

- **Authorship (traditional attribution to Jeremiah).** No challenge item in this brief asserts
  authorship as a testable claim, matching the medium brief's bounding — the book is presented only
  as "Lamentations" in every item above.
- **Lamentations 3:1-17, excluded affliction imagery.** No challenge-ready verse in this brief
  draws on this range — items 4-5 and 9 open at 3:18 or later, exactly matching the medium brief's
  passage boundary; the boss sequence's narrative labels (item 7) likewise stay at 3:18 and later,
  with no reference to the more visceral imagery preceding it. **Exclusion holds, unchanged.**
- **Lamentations 2:20 and 4:10, cannibalism during the siege.** Not selected as a pivotal passage
  and no verse from either is challenge-ready text anywhere in this brief. **Exclusion holds,
  unchanged.**
- **Lamentations 5:22, the book's actual final verse.** The closing-plea passage stops at 5:21 in
  this brief (item 6 and the boss sequence's final beat), exactly matching the medium brief's
  deliberate boundary — 5:22 is not used as challenge-ready text, for the same live-translation-
  ambiguity reason the medium review documented. **Exclusion holds, unchanged.**
- **The book's function in Jewish (Tisha B'Av) and Christian (Tenebrae) liturgy.** No challenge
  item or scene description makes a liturgical or typological claim in either direction; every item
  tests only the book's own text, matching the medium brief's bounding.
- **No new contested ground otherwise** — every item is plain verbatim recall of already-reviewed
  text, with no live cross-tradition disagreement bearing on what's graded.

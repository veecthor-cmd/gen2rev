## Design intent for this tier

Per Kachi: "hard... needs very deep scripture knowledge." This tier draws on verses within the same
7 approved passages that are theologically or narratively significant but less likely to be
immediately recognized — precise closing clauses, dating details, secondary phrasing — and requires
typed recall (no multiple-choice scaffolding) wherever the format allows. It stays inside the same
guardrails as every other tier: verbatim WEB text only, narrative fact only, no doctrinal position
required. All items are `difficulty_rank` 5.

## Pivotal Passages — reused from `docs/content/zechariah.md`, no new passages added

Same 7 passages as `medium` and `easy`, all from chapters 1-8: Zechariah 1:1-6, 3:1-5, 4:1-7, 5:1-4,
7:8-10, 8:3-8, 8:16-17. **Chapters 9-14 remain entirely out of scope at this tier, same as every
other tier** — see Contested Territory below.

## Challenge Items (10: 7 regular + 3 boss)

### Regular (7)

1. **Zechariah 1:1** (WEB, new): "In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, Yahweh's word
   came to the prophet Zechariah the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo, saying," — recall: "In the
   eighth month, in the second year of ___, Yahweh's word came to the prophet Zechariah the son of
   Berechiah, the son of Iddo, saying," → **Darius**. *(The book's own dating notice — precise and
   rarely memorized compared to the oracles that follow it.)*
2. **Zechariah 1:5** (WEB, new): "Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, do they live
   forever?" — recall: "Your fathers, where are they? And the ___, do they live forever?" →
   **prophets**.
3. **Zechariah 3:2** (WEB, new): "Yahweh said to Satan, 'Yahweh rebuke you, Satan! Yes, Yahweh who
   has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Isn't this a burning stick plucked out of the fire?'" — recall:
   "Yahweh said to Satan, 'Yahweh rebuke you, Satan! Yes, Yahweh who has chosen ___ rebuke you!...'"
   → **Jerusalem**. *(Narrative fact only — the rebuke itself and its stated object, Jerusalem, per
   the existing "Satan" bounding below; no item asks what "Satan" is or does beyond this line's own
   words.)*
4. **Zechariah 3:4** (already-approved verse, different clause than the `medium` item drawn from the
   same verse — `medium` used this verse's first sentence in MC format, "what did the angel tell the
   attendants to do"; this item uses the second sentence instead): "He answered and spoke to those
   who stood before him, saying, 'Take the filthy garments off him.' To him he said, 'Behold, I have
   caused your iniquity to pass from you, and I will clothe you with rich clothing.'" — recall: "...I
   have caused your iniquity to pass from you, and I will clothe you with ___ clothing." → **rich**.
5. **Zechariah 4:7** (WEB, new): "Who are you, great mountain? Before Zerubbabel you are a plain; and
   he will bring out the capstone with shouts of Grace, grace, to it!" — recall: "...and he will
   bring out the capstone with shouts of ___, ___, to it!" → **Grace, grace**. *(The verse
   immediately following the book's most-quoted line, itself far less commonly recalled.)*
6. **Zechariah 5:4** (WEB, new): "'I will cause it to go out,' says Yahweh of Armies, 'and it will
   enter into the house of the thief, and into the house of him who swears falsely by my name; and
   it will remain in the middle of his house, and will destroy it with its timber and its stones.'"
   — recall: "...and will destroy it with its timber and its ___." → **stones**.
7. **Zechariah 8:6** (WEB, new; trimmed for challenge use — full verse: "Yahweh of Armies says: 'If
   it is marvelous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, should it also be
   marvelous in my eyes?' says Yahweh of Armies."): "If it is marvelous in the eyes of the remnant of
   this people in those days, should it also be marvelous in my eyes?" — recall: "If it is marvelous
   in the eyes of the remnant of this people in those days, should it also be ___ in my eyes?" →
   **marvelous**. *(Rhetorical self-reference by Yahweh — not a pronoun-ambiguity or identity
   question of the kind flagged elsewhere in this project; confirmed plain narrative rhetoric, see
   Contested Territory below.)*

### Boss (3)

8. **Zechariah 1:6** (WEB, new): "But my words and my decrees, which I commanded my servants the
   prophets, didn't they overtake your fathers? Then they repented and said, 'Just as Yahweh of
   Armies determined to do to us, according to our ways and according to our practices, so he has
   dealt with us.'" — recall: "'Just as Yahweh of Armies determined to do to us, according to our
   ways and according to our ___, so he has dealt with us.'" → **practices**.
9. **Boss sequence — reused verbatim from `medium`**: Put these seven pivotal passages in the order
   they appear in Zechariah 1-8 (1:1-6 → 3:1-5 → 4:1-7 → 5:1-4 → 7:8-10 → 8:3-8 → 8:16-17).
10. **Zechariah 8:8** (WEB, new): "'I will bring them, and they will dwell within Jerusalem. They
    will be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.'" — recall: "They
    will be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in ___." → **righteousness**. *(The
    closing line of the restoration oracle — a fitting recall-tier capstone for this book's boss
    battle, precise and less commonly quoted than the "east country/west country" clause `medium`
    already used from the same passage.)*

## Contested Territory

Everything already flagged in `docs/content/zechariah.md` applies unchanged and is **re-confirmed to
hold for this tier**:

- **No verse from Zechariah 9:9, 11:12-13, 12:10, or 13:7 appears anywhere in this brief.** No item
  above draws on any material from chapters 9-14.
- **No verse from Zechariah 3:8-10 or 6:12-13 ("the Branch") appears anywhere in this brief.** Items
  3-4 stop, like `medium` and `easy`, at 3:2 and 3:4 respectively — the vision's "Branch" material
  (3:8-10) is never reached.
- **Zechariah 3:2 (item 3) and 3:1's "Satan" reference** (only referenced by name within item 3's
  own quoted verse) stay narrative-only, per the existing bounding in `docs/reviews/zechariah-review.md`
  §3.7 — the item grades only that Yahweh rebukes Satan and names Jerusalem as the reason, never a
  characterization of who or what "Satan" is beyond the text's own narration.
- **Zechariah 8:6 (item 7)** — checked independently for this brief: Yahweh's rhetorical
  self-reference ("should it also be marvelous in my eyes?") is plain first-person prophetic speech,
  not a pronoun-ambiguity or Trinity-adjacent question of the kind flagged for Genesis 1:26 elsewhere
  in this project. No live cross-tradition disagreement found or expected — this is a straightforward
  rhetorical question about Yahweh's own reaction, addressed to no disputed referent.
- **No new contested ground otherwise.** Every other newly-selected verse in this brief (1:1, 1:5,
  1:6, 3:4's second clause, 4:7, 5:4, 8:8) is plain narrative description, dating detail, or direct
  quotation with no live cross-tradition disagreement identified.

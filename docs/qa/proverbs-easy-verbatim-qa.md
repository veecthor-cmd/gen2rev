## Corrections

- **Item 1 — verse-reference discrepancy (not a wording error).** The brief quoted the full two-sentence
  text of Prov 3:5-6 ("Trust in Yahweh with all your heart, and don't lean on your own understanding. In
  all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight.") but cited it only as "Prov
  3:5" — verse 5 alone is only the first sentence; the quoted text's second sentence is verse 6. The
  original medium-tier brief (`docs/content/proverbs.md`) cites this same full text correctly as
  "3:5-6," and this easy-tier brief's own boss item 12 cites the identical text correctly as "3:5-6" —
  item 1's "3:5" was the outlier. **Fixed in `docs/content/proverbs-easy.md`: citation widened to
  "Prov 3:5-6."** No word of the quoted text itself was wrong.

## Correction made

**Item 2, Jer 1:18** — `docs/content/jeremiah-hard.md` originally read (full quote): "'For, behold,
I have made you today a fortified city, and an iron pillar, and bronze walls, against the whole
land, against the kings of Judah, against its princes, against its priests, and against the people
of the land.'" and (recall stem): "I have made you today a fortified city, and an iron pillar, and
bronze ___." → walls.

Corrected to match the verified WEB text exactly:
- Full quote: "'For behold, I have made you today a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze
  walls against the whole land—against the kings of Judah, against its princes, against its
  priests, and against the people of the land.'"
- Recall stem: "I have made you today a fortified city, an iron pillar, and bronze ___." → **walls**
  (blank and answer unchanged; only the surrounding text was corrected).

This is a real wording discrepancy, not a formatting or quote-nesting note — the original brief's
extra comma after "For" and extra "and" before "an iron pillar" changed the sentence's punctuation
and word count from actual WEB text, despite the brief's claim of "exact match between both
[ebible.org and biblegateway.com]." Both of those sources were re-checked directly during this pass
and both, in fact, match Bible Hub and each other — the discrepancy was introduced during the
brief's own transcription, not a disagreement between sources. Fixed directly in the content file;
citation (Jer 1:18, WEB) left unchanged.

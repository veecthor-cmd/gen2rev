## Item 8 — specific verse-boundary check (per the review's explicit QA instruction)

The theological review's Finding A caught that the original draft's item 8 presented "I set my
rainbow in the cloud, and it will be a sign of a covenant between me and the earth, for perpetual
___" as if it were one verse (cited "Gen 9:13") — a string that exists nowhere in WEB, since "for
perpetual generations" is actually the tail of **9:12**, occurring *before* 9:13's clause, not after
it.

Re-verified independently: the corrected brief now cites Gen 9:12 alone, with the blank on
"generations" falling inside that single verse's own text, confirmed above. No composite-quotation
or wrong-citation issue remains. **No other item in this tier's brief cuts a blank from a multi-verse
block** — item 3 (Gen 3:19) trims within a single verse and is clearly labelled as such; every other
item is a single, complete verse.

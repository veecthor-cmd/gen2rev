## Discrepancy found and fixed — Item 9 (Ezek 37:14)

The brief quoted Ezek 37:14 as: *"I will put my Spirit in you, and you will live. Then I will place
you in your own land; and you will know that I, Yahweh, have spoken it and performed it."* — with
no trim label, presented as if this were the complete verse.

Both Bible Hub and ebible.org confirm the actual full verse continues: *"...have spoken it and
performed it," says Yahweh.'* — the brief silently dropped the closing `, says Yahweh.` clause. This
is a silent truncation, not a labeled trim (every other trimmed item in this brief — e.g. items 1 and
7 — explicitly says "trimmed for challenge use, full verse: ...").

**Fix applied directly to `docs/content/ezekiel-hard.md`, item 9:** added the trim label
`(WEB, already-approved verse, new format; trimmed for challenge use, full verse continues "...says
Yahweh.")`, matching this brief's own convention for every other trimmed verse. No wording in the
quoted challenge text or the recall blank/answer was changed — the fix is a labeling correction, not
a content correction, since the quoted portion itself was already verbatim-correct as a prefix.

## Discrepancies found

None that affect wording, word order, verse numbering, or misrepresent what the source says. Three
cosmetic/completeness notes recorded above:
- Josh 4:6-7: inner quotation marks present in source, omitted in brief's rendering (words identical).
- Josh 2:1 and Josh 6:3-5: brief's quote stops mid-verse at a natural clause boundary without an
  ellipsis marker, while other trimmed entries in this same brief do use ellipses. The text actually
  quoted in both cases is word-for-word accurate to source — this is a citation-formatting
  inconsistency, not a textual-accuracy problem, so no correction was made to the brief's wording.

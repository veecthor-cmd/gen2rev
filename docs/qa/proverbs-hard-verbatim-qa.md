## Corrections

- **Item 13 — verse-reference discrepancy (not a wording error).** The brief quoted only "For her good
  profit is better than getting silver, and her return is better than fine gold." — this is Proverbs
  3:14 alone (confirmed against Bible Hub) — but cited it as "Prov 3:13-15," a three-verse range. The
  3:13-15 range citation is correct for the *combined* passage as quoted in the original medium-tier
  brief (`docs/content/proverbs.md`, which quotes all of vv.13-15 together), but this hard-tier item
  quotes only the middle verse (14) at its own new blank position, so citing the full three-verse range
  overstates what's actually quoted here. **Fixed in `docs/content/proverbs-hard.md`: citation narrowed
  to "Prov 3:14."** No word of the quoted text itself was wrong.
